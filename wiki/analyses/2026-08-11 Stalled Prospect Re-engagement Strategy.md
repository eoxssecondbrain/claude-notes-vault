# Stalled Prospect Re-engagement Strategy

## Question

Which of the stalled prospects should we prioritize for re-engagement, and what specific action should we take with each?

## Executive Summary

**Priority 1 (Act Now):** Primrose Alloys — highest investment already sunk; re-engagement has highest ROI  
**Priority 2 (This Month):** Pegasus Steel — warm but idle; easy re-activation  
**Priority 3 (Nurture):** Ace Steel Supply — re-engaged recently; steady progress  
**Priority 4 (Close-Out):** Noble Metal — respectfully decline further pursuit

## Findings

### **TIER 1: HIGHEST PRIORITY — PRIMROSE ALLOYS**

**Status:** Stalled since Feb 27, 2026 (5+ months)  
**Investment to Date:** Multiple sales calls (3 Fathom calls Jan 15–16), CFO-level sandbox onboarding, logins provisioned  
**Why It Matters:** This is EOXS's most advanced stalled deal; they've moved past initial discovery into sandbox evaluation.

#### **The Problem**
- Last documented activity: **Feb 27, 2026** — impromptu Zoom meeting (10 minutes per Fathom recap)
- No emails after Feb 13 in the archive; no subsequent calls documented
- **Ayan Dutta** (EOXS's technical contact/champion) went on casual leave June 24–26, 2026, per batch 18
- No documented close-out: deal status unknown (stalled, lost, or slow-moving)

#### **Recommended Action**
1. **Immediate:** Raj to send a brief, non-pressured check-in email to **David Kim** (CFO) within 48 hours.  
   *Tone:* "We haven't connected since our Zoom in late February. I wanted to circle back and see if you've had any progress on your internal evaluation or if there's anything we can clarify about the product before moving forward."  
   *Purpose:* Establish whether the deal is alive, stalled, or dead. If dead, confirm it and document closure.

2. **If David responds positively:** Schedule a 30–45 minute call with **David + Ayan** (after Ayan returns from leave) to:
   - Review any feedback from their internal evaluation
   - Address pain points or feature gaps
   - Move from evaluation to either a pilot deployment or close the deal
   - Timeline: Target a call within 1–2 weeks of first contact

3. **If no response after 7 days:** Send one follow-up (no more than 1) with explicit ask: "If this isn't the right time, no problem—just let us know and we can pick it back up down the road."  
   *Then mark as "Lost/Paused" in CRM and move to quarterly check-in cadence.*

**Success Metric:** Re-establish communication and get a go/no-go decision within 30 days.

**Success Metric:** Either lock an evaluation agreement within 14 days, or pause pursuit.
