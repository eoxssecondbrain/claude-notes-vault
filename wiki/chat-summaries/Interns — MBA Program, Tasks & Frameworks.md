# Interns — MBA Program, Tasks & Frameworks

_One-sentence description of what this cluster of conversations covers._

Raj's design and management of a 50-person MBA-fresher intern cohort joining EOXS on 1 August 2026 — the founding "MBA vs non-MBA" narrative behind it, the task-lifecycle/QA architecture being built for them, an email-marketing task framework, and a real mid-program conflict when a sub-group tried to redefine their assigned task.

## Summary

The intern program originates from personal narrative, not conventional HR planning. Across two near-duplicate threads (2026-07-29 and 2026-07-31), Raj describes wanting to open the interns' first day by framing it as himself (a self-made, non-MBA, factory-floor-to-founder story) versus 50 MBA freshers, competing head-to-head on revenue generated over six months — an explicit attempt to "rip apart" what he calls the Indian cultural belief system around MBA credentials. In both threads the assistant pushed back on the same three grounds: (1) unfair baseline — Raj has ~20,000 LinkedIn connections and 10 years of steel-industry relationship equity a 22-year-old fresher cannot match, so a head-to-head "win" would prove nothing about MBA vs non-MBA; (2) attribution — EOXS's sales motion is founder-led/relationship-driven, so crediting a closed deal to one intern's six months of activity is not clean; (3) closed-revenue-as-metric is the wrong yardstick for freshers — pipeline-contribution metrics (qualified leads sourced, meetings booked, demo requests, content/webinar reach) are realistic and attributable per intern via the task manager, whereas closed-dollar revenue is not. The assistant's recommendation, given twice, independently: reframe day one from "beat my number" to "here's what good contribution looks like, and it's tracked," and hold any PR/recruiting story until six months in, with a real defensible number — publicizing before then risks a credibility problem in a small, relationship-driven industry.

On 2026-08-04, Raj moved into program mechanics: an 8–12 stage task lifecycle for the intern task list, with three human QA layers per task and payment tied to task completion. The assistant flagged, before building anything, that three human QA layers at even modest volume (3 tasks/intern/week × 50 interns = 150 tasks/week, 450 review actions/week) is the single highest-risk element — it can jam the whole board at QA and stop interns from getting paid, undermining the incentive mechanic itself. The assistant asked for five inputs before designing stages: system of record (ERP task manager vs. separate tracker), whether stages model task lifecycle or intern onboarding journey, the three named QA reviewers, incentive mechanics (per-task bounty vs. stipend-as-pool, and who holds payment-approval authority), and expected task volume. No stage list had been finalized as of this thread — it ends on the assistant offering three build-depth options (5ft/10ft/50ft) pending Raj's answers.

On 2026-08-03, Raj proposed an email-marketing task for the interns: source CEOs of 50–100 employee companies via ZoomInfo and run mass outbound. The assistant flagged that "CEO of a 50-100 person company" is a weak proxy for actual ICP fit (steel/metals distributor) and asked Raj to pick a targeting filter before interns start building sequences. The thread ends inconclusively — Raj sent a TikTok link ("this is how we do it") as a reference example that the assistant could not resolve (short-link format rejected by the transcription tool, direct fetch blocked by TikTok's robots policy), and the thread cuts off before the reference was clarified.

The most concrete, dated event in the cluster is 2026-08-17: a named intern (Prapti) and her group proposed a "Test Case Bank" task — writing test queries for AskCruz (an internal AI product/assistant) and grading its answers as correct/incorrect — instead of their assigned content-creation task. Raj rejected it and had the assistant draft a pointed written response, sent as-is, which is captured verbatim in the raw transcript.

## Key Points
- 50 MBA-fresher interns start 1 August 2026 (per the 2026-07-31 thread); an earlier thread (2026-07-29) references the same idea with "15 interns," suggesting the cohort size was still being finalized as late as two days before the later, larger number was set.
- The assistant's core objection to a founder-vs-interns revenue contest, made independently in both 2026-07-29 and 2026-07-31 threads: Raj's ~20,000 LinkedIn connections and 10 years of relationship equity make any "win" meaningless as a test of MBA vs non-MBA capability.
- Recommended metric for interns is pipeline contribution (qualified leads, meetings booked, demo requests, content/webinar reach) tracked per-intern in the task manager — not closed-dollar revenue.
- PR/recruiting story ("we ran a 50-person fresher internship and generated $X") should be held until after the six months, with a verified, defensible number — publicizing early risks credibility in steel industry's relationship-driven, small-world market.
- 2026-08-04: task lifecycle design calls for 8–12 stages, three human QA layers, and task completion = payment triggered. Assistant flagged 450 weekly review actions (at 150 tasks/week for 50 interns) as a payment-blocking bottleneck risk before any stage list was built. No stages were finalized in this thread.
- 2026-08-03: email marketing task framework proposed — ZoomInfo-sourced list of CEOs at 50-100 employee companies, to be assigned to interns as mass-email task. Assistant flagged ICP-fit gap (company-size proxy alone doesn't confirm steel/metals relevance). Thread ends unresolved on an unretrievable TikTok reference link.
- 2026-08-17: Raj rejected the "Test Case Bank" proposal from Prapti's group outright and had a written rebuke drafted and sent, reasserting content creation as their required task and explicitly linking task-eligibility to demonstrated product/ICP knowledge.
