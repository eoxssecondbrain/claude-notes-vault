# Team Performance Reviews & Raise Requests

_A three-week run of Raj-driven performance investigations and raise decisions (Dhrup, Ron, Hashir), plus adjacent weekly-performance pulls (Ayan, Isha, Harsimran) and Ron's eventual departure on 2026-08-17._

## Summary

Between 2026-07-27 and 2026-08-17, Raj used Claude repeatedly to build "50ft deep" evidence packets before raise/scope conversations with three team members — Dhrup Kumar Singh, Ron J, and Hashir Saleem — pulling from OV2 wiki pages, live EOXS Teams (Odoo) task data, Cattr productivity tracking, payroll registers, and email threads.

**Dhrup Kumar Singh** was reviewed twice in three days: once for a possible role/scope change (2026-07-31) and once after he directly asked Raj for a raise (2026-08-01). Both reviews landed on the same verdict — real, foundational strength (he authored all three of EOXS's core engineering SOPs — GitLab branching, code review/QA, dev standards — in one day, 2026-06-30) undercut by a declining Cattr productivity trend (42.7% down to 27.2% over five weeks, 87% late logins in June) and, most importantly, an unanswered direct ask: Raj requested a 6-month team-growth plan on 2026-07-25, followed up with a bare "??" on 2026-07-31, still unanswered as of 2026-08-01. Dhrup is on a signed 10-year comp ladder (Senior Product Developer, EOXS dba Prata Inc) explicitly marked "fixed and non-negotiable" in the contract, running INR 60K (2024) to a fixed INR 90K/mo 2027–2034, plus incentive/milestone bonuses and a 5-year non-compete. Claude's recommendation both times: resolve the unanswered emails before discussing money, and treat SOP-authorship as the strongest case for expanded technical/architectural authority — not for a bigger direct-report headcount, which is the specific thing he's currently non-responsive on.

**Ron J**'s raise request (2026-07-30/31 thread) got the same treatment. Ron holds outsized structural authority for his title — sole production-deployment approval gate per the GitLab Branching SOP (nothing ships to any client without his sign-off), sole org-wide leave approver, and primary relationship lead on Sabre Alloys, 3GM Steel, and Ohio Strip Steel. Pay had already risen from ~₹20K/mo (late 2023) to ₹40–45K/mo after a March 2026 raise — four months before this ask. Cattr showed 25–31% weekly productivity and 54% late logins in June, and — the sharpest single data point in this whole cluster — an escalation-bot reply from Ron himself on an 8-month-old bug: "Not important. But, assign this to someone so that we stop getting this notification every day," which is close to a verbatim match for the exact behavior Raj's company-wide PM-discipline email (sent 2026-07-31) was written to correct. Claude also flagged, unprompted, that Ron is Raj's first cousin — framed as a reason for *more* process rigor, not less, in either direction. Recommendation: address the tracker-hygiene pattern directly and separately from money, then frame any raise as scope-based (his de facto job is closer to "technical operations lead" than "implementation consultant," and likely underpriced against market for that scope) rather than as a reward for the ask itself.

**Hashir Saleem**'s request (2026-08-03) was framed by Claude as a restoration case, not a merit case: his pay fell roughly 50% from a Sep 2025 peak of ₹43,400/mo to ₹14,520–31,692/mo through mid-2026, tracking a company-wide payroll contraction (EOXS average monthly payroll spend fell from $14,691 in 2025 to $7,950 by mid-2026), not a performance action. His Cattr productivity score (21–36%) was explicitly flagged as unreliable evidence in either direction due to a known, still-unresolved hour-under-capture bug (the same bug that cost Remya 70%+ of logged hours at one point). Market-rate comparisons for an India-based Odoo/ERP developer supported roughly ₹35,000–45,000/month for his actual scope of work (Sabre Alloys traceability fix, PPC Metals $373K valuation-discrepancy writeup, DPS journal-entry anomalies) — restoring him to his Sep 2025 level, not above it.

Adjacent threads: a 2026-07-27 pull on Ayan Dutta's and Isha Bisht's weekly EOXS-Teams activity found essentially no usable signal — zero hours logged, zero attendance records, and for Isha zero assigned tasks at all in that Odoo tenant — which Claude called out as a data-hygiene problem in its own right, not just a null result. A 2026-08-06 "tell me everything about Ayan" request stalled at the rigor-level clarifying question and was never completed in this transcript. A 2026-07-28 thread had Ayan iteratively drafting (via Claude) an incentive request to Isha/Raj — ₹50,000 for a Brannon upsell (project value $2,700 USD) and ₹10,000 for converting cold lead "Collier Metals" into a discovery call — notable mainly for the currency mixup Claude initially made (assumed both figures were USD before Ayan corrected: incentives are INR, deal value is USD). A 2026-07-28 pull on Harsimran Singh's weekly report showed a productivity drop to 40.5% (from a 50–58% range over the prior five weeks) while hours stayed roughly on pattern (35.1h worked vs. 37.5h expected), prompting three drafted check-in email variants.

Finally, on 2026-08-17, Raj had Claude chart Ron's and Sheenam's monthly logged hours from Jan 2025–Jul 2026 (Sheenam's hours cratered to 62h then 39h in Feb–Mar 2026 before recovering — flagged by Claude as worth a "gut check"), and on the same day asked Claude to draft a group departure announcement for Ron, whose last day was set as that Friday. No reason for the departure is stated in the transcript.

## Tribal Knowledge Extracted
- **"50ft deep" / "10ft deep" / "5ft deep"** — Raj's own informal rigor scale for how thorough a Claude investigation should be before a personnel decision (5ft = quick pass from available records, 10ft = verified against underlying records, 50ft = full investigation including adjacent risks/blind spots). Used consistently across the Dhrup, Ron, and Hashir raise reviews as a shorthand Raj expects Claude to recognize without explanation. (source: raj_2026-07-31_dhrup-kumar-singh-performance-review.md, raj_2026-08-01_dhrup-raise-review.md, raj_2026-07-30_erp-tickets-dhrup-motivation-strategy.md, raj_2026-08-03_hashir-raise-request.md)
- **Dhrup's comp ladder is contractually "fixed and non-negotiable" through 2034** — a signed 10-year salary schedule (Senior Product Developer, EOXS dba Prata Inc) explicitly using that phrase in the contract text itself. This is an unwritten constraint on any raise conversation with him: granting an off-ladder increase effectively voids his own contract's central term, which Claude flagged as something Raj needs to resolve explicitly (off-ladder raise vs. accelerating the already-scheduled 2027 jump) rather than treat as a normal raise ask. (source: raj_2026-08-01_dhrup-raise-review.md)
- **Cattr's productivity percentage is known-unreliable and should not be read as a clean signal** — flagged independently in both the Ron and Hashir reviews as having a documented data-quality bug (a uniform 22-hour baseline miscalibration in May 2026 that generated inaccurate flags company-wide, plus a separate still-unresolved hour-under-capture bug dating to at least May 2025 that once cost employee Remya 70%+ of her logged hours). Raj has already overridden a Cattr flag once before (for Hashir, July 2026) without leaving a documented reason — Claude called this out as a real gap if it becomes a pattern others notice. Treat any bare Cattr productivity % cited elsewhere in this vault as directional at best, not evidence. (source: raj_2026-07-30_erp-tickets-dhrup-motivation-strategy.md, raj_2026-08-03_hashir-raise-request.md)
- **The company-wide PM-discipline email Raj sent 2026-07-31** (about tasks sitting unresolved past 90 days, one over 300 days with no response to nudges) directly and specifically describes Dhrup's own stalled task list and Ron's own "not important, assign to someone else" reply to an aging bug — both reviews explicitly tie the general memo back to the specific person being reviewed, meaning any future raise granted without addressing that email's standard would be read internally as inconsistent. (source: raj_2026-07-31_dhrup-kumar-singh-performance-review.md, raj_2026-07-30_erp-tickets-dhrup-motivation-strategy.md)
- **Ron J is Raj's first cousin** — an undocumented-elsewhere family relationship that Claude surfaced unprompted during the raise review, framed as a reason to apply more rather than less rigor to his case (avoid both underpaying him because "family shouldn't need incentives" and fast-tracking him past the standard other employees get). (source: raj_2026-07-30_erp-tickets-dhrup-motivation-strategy.md)
- **task-level effort/time fields (planned_hours, effective_hours) are effectively unused in EOXS Teams/Odoo** — Dhrup's entire 44-task load shows 0 in both fields, meaning task duration in the system cannot currently be cross-checked against actual time invested; a structural blind spot for any efficiency judgment, not specific to Dhrup. (source: raj_2026-07-31_dhrup-kumar-singh-performance-review.md)
- **Isha Bisht has zero footprint in the EOXS Teams (Odoo) instance** — no assigned tasks ever, no attendance records — meaning if she's doing real work, it isn't tracked through that system at all; anyone checking her activity needs to go to Gmail/Fireflies/OV2 instead, not Odoo. (source: raj_2026-07-27_ayan-weekly-performance-eoxs-teams.md)
- **Ayan Dutta's incentive-request amounts are denominated in mixed currency by convention** — incentive payouts requested in INR (₹), underlying deal/project values cited in USD ($) — a distinction Claude got wrong on first draft and Ayan had to correct; worth remembering as the house convention rather than assuming both figures share a currency next time. (source: ayan_2026-07-28_ayan-incentive-request-rephrase.md)
