# AskCruz — Ops, Scrum & Team Reporting (Aug 17)

_A dense single-day cluster covering AskCruz's Odoo task-board scrum reporting (daily + weekly, scheduled and ad hoc), a founder payout tracker spec, an intern's security proposal review, onboarding/culture doctrine, and one sales call-prep thread._

## Summary

On 2026-08-17 Raj ran (or had scheduled) two parallel scrum-reporting builds against the `teams-askcruz` Odoo project board (project_id=6, "AskCruz" project, 39 active tasks across stages Tasks/Assigned/QA/Completed/Tabled). A scheduled daily report and a scheduled weekly report both fired automatically and independently arrived at the same headline finding: essentially all board activity — every log note and every stage move — was authored by Raj himself, chasing non-responsive assignees (Ron, Ayan Dutta, Sheenam), not by the assignees self-reporting. The daily report (window: today only) found 27 notes and 2 stage moves, all by Raj; zero team members posted anything themselves. The weekly report (window: Aug 11–17) scored team members out of 10 — Sheenam 5.6, Ron 3, Ayan Dutta 1.4, Rajat Jain/Nidhi Rana/Yashvir Singh Thakur 0 — and flagged that Ron's "100% acknowledgment" score was an artifact of the metric not checking authorship (every note on his tasks was Raj chasing him, not Ron responding). A separate ad hoc conversation (`askcruz-daily-task-board-scrum`) had Raj co-designing the scoring rubric interactively before the scheduled versions existed, settling on "50ft full scrum-master audit" rigor and "any log note counts as acknowledgment," and surfaced that 26 of 28 open tasks have no deadline at all, and Ayan's queue has 6 of 8 tasks stuck in the earliest "Tasks" column untouched for 9+ days. A follow-up thread confirmed the weekly report's email send succeeded (Gmail message ID returned) but Raj couldn't locate it in his inbox — traced to the session's Gmail connector possibly being authenticated as a different account (nidhi@eoxsteam.com) rather than Raj's own mailbox, and to the connector lacking any search/read tools (send-only).

Beyond scrum mechanics: Raj killed the open-web AskCruz frontend build (moving to "Claude frontend" plus a per-customer Pro-vs-Enterprise evaluation) and asked for related conversation history to be archived/suppressed from future surfacing. He dictated culture doctrine (reason-vs-fear energy, Map of Consciousness) with a concrete example — asking "did you do this task?" and getting a defensive ramble back is fear energy — to be built into the onboarding doc, alongside notes that Cattr is being integrated directly with Discord to fix reliability issues, and that the task-tracker SOP needs a full step-by-step rewrite because the real problem is lack of onboarding, not lack of policy. A new-hire MBA-team member, Jaee Moharir (offer accepted Aug 3, 2026), authored an unprompted "Cruz Trust Layer" security/governance case study; Claude reviewed it as strong conceptual thinking but not build-ready, found no implementation-task track record for her in the system, and recommended a scoped gap-analysis deliverable (reconcile against the existing ABAC design and an in-progress security audit) as the evidence needed before deciding whether she leads it solo — Raj had feedback and a next-step email drafted for her without it costing him review time. A founder payout tracker spec (Ron $10M / Sheenam $5M commitments against a 260-week hours-based accrual model, base 40hrs/week + up to 20hrs/week founder premium) was reviewed for logic: the "first rights order" language was flagged as ambiguous between sequential waterfall and parallel accrual (Raj confirmed parallel), and the agreement was confirmed **not yet signed** by Ron or Sheenam — so no real dollar figures should go out to them yet. A devops-structure thread and a Pacesetter/Steve Leebow AskCruz sales call-prep thread round out the day; the devops thread stalled on a mid-sentence trailing-off message. A 100ft-rigor LinkedIn strategy thread concluded Raj's actual pipeline (Sabre Alloys, 3GM Steel, Collier Metals, Bossard, IMS Metals) is 100% steel/metals despite AskCruz's horizontal public positioning, and recommended resolving that mismatch before writing content pillars.
