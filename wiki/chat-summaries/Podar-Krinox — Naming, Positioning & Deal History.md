# Podar-Krinox — Naming, Positioning & Deal History

_One-sentence description of what this cluster of conversations covers._

Ten conversations across 2026-07-27 to 2026-07-29 tracking GFC's flagship AI product from "Podar" through "Krinox" to "Cruz," in parallel with its website messaging/positioning work, its first live deal (Ryan Capinski/Eastern States Steel), and its prospect list.

## Summary

GFC's flagship AI operating system product started this cluster named **Podar**, was renamed **Krinox** on 2026-07-27 (memory-edit #8 logged that day), and was ultimately abandoned in favor of **Cruz** by the end of the 2026-07-29 messaging session — a three-name arc that happened entirely within this three-day window and is not yet reflected anywhere in OV2. GFC ownership: Raj 51%, Ron 20% (product/implementation), Ayan 20% (engineering/AI architecture), 9% ESOP; separate legal entity from Prata Inc/EOXS, leaning LLC (DE or WY); Raj contributed $50K + IRIS IP (IRIS itself was acquired from EOXS into GFC for $10K). Product architecture is described as an 8-layer vision: Company Brain → Company Hands → Digital Workforce → Generic Intelligence → Professional/Human DNA → Company Personalization → Marketplace → Continuous Learning, built as a superset of OV + IRIS + Claude ingesting live ERP, email, call transcripts, and static KB.

Sales strategy discussion (07-27) ranked the top deal-killing risks as: (1) buy-vs-build objection (Ram Shenoy already raised "why not build ourselves"), (2) security/access-control credibility (OV has no native permissioning, against the backdrop of EOXS's plaintext-credential history and the Feb 2026 Sabre breach), (3) brand confusion (zero independent track record), (4) unproven platform+per-agent pricing, (5) CloudForge category-timing risk. A follow-up search (07-27) confirmed no other conversation or OV2 record ties data protection specifically to Krinox — it remains an open, unaddressed gap.

The website-messaging work ran across three sessions (07-28 ×2, 07-29) using a "council" framework (Category Positioning Strategist, B2B SaaS Messaging Strategist, ICP/Buyer Psychology Expert, Website Conversion Strategist, Brand Voice & Copy Director) modeled on agency Emotive Brand. Locked decisions: CTA is "Book a fit call" (not a waitlist — reversed after Raj revealed the product is fully built and the goal is $1M revenue in 3 months via ~30 customers at ~$30K ACV); emotional core is "competence undermined by bad information, craving to feel in command again"; persona is "trusted senior advisor"; proof strategy is "steel as credibility weapon" (built inside one of the most fragmented, undocumented industries in American manufacturing, framed as evidence of range for a horizontal ICP of CEO/owners of 50-100-employee mid-market companies). A full 15-section website blueprint and a detailed dark-palette/typography/motion design spec were produced and locked on 07-28.

Naming consumed the largest share of this cluster — eleven brainstorm rounds across three sessions plus a dedicated name-search spreadsheet pass. Krinox was initially locked and Raj said he'd buy krinox.com, but was later rejected because Raj himself kept forgetting the name. Extensive mythological/historical/character naming rounds (Odin, Raven, Radar, Sherlock, Polanyi, Playfair, Munim, Paras, etc.) were run and progressively rejected on collision, legal (Yoda ruled out — active Disney trademark), or memorability grounds. On 07-29 Raj proposed **Cruz** (askcruz.com, available on GoDaddy for ~$12) and, after Claude raised and Raj rebutted five objections (Databahn's existing "Cruz" AI data product, weak surname trademark strength, owning only askcruz.com not cruz.com, celebrity/political association, SEO weakness), Claude reversed its recommendation and endorsed Cruz — reasoning that Raj's GTM is cold-calling and content/IG-driven, not SEO-driven, so memorability beats searchability. This also produced a late-session reframe: dropping "AI operating system" language from the customer-facing homepage entirely (keep it only for the investor page) in favor of buyer-language specificity ("Cruz has read every order, every email, and every call in your business. Ask him anything").

Separately, Raj had a live call with Ryan Capinski (Eastern States Steel) on 07-27 pitching Krinox for freight/load optimization at $15,000 one-time implementation + $1,500/month, with implementation-cost and labor discounts offered to close fast, tied to a hard deadline: get it running before next month's SMU conference, where Raj offered to buy Ryan a $1,300 ticket to showcase the product. On 07-28, a CRM-based prospect list was pulled for Krinox outreach (Coilplus, Horizon Steel, Modesto Steel, Steel Warehouse/Ted Lerman, HarbisonWalker/Crawford Murton, Titanium Industries, Monarch Steel/Marty Socha, FerroSource/Daniel Bain, Ace Steel Supply) — Horizon Steel was explicitly removed per Raj's instruction ("horizon steel is an asshole, we don't want to do business with them"), FerroSource and Ace Steel are marked LOST in CRM, and Monarch's CRM stage ("Unsure," last touch 2025-08-07) was flagged as stale/wrong since Raj's own context has Monarch already live on a Podar-style AI email-to-Invera AR/AP agent. A separate 07-27 thread began routing Krinox's LinkedIn/content strategy through the 50-person MBA intern program but was explicitly deferred at Raj's instruction ("don't do anything right now").
