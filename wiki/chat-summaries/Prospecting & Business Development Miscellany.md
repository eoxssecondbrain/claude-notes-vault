# Prospecting & Business Development Miscellany

_A cluster of loosely-related sales/BD threads — a stalled ERP deal revived via a new angle, an internal product-naming mix-up, branding judgment on using a prospect's private email as marketing content, an unfinished pricing framing exercise, an unresolved brainstorm request, and a landing-page psychology discussion — grouped here because none forms its own topic cluster.

## Summary

The most substantial thread (2026-07-29) is a full "council" analysis of the Bri-Steel Manufacturing deal, a seamless-pipe manufacturer (Edmonton/Enid) that went LOST in CRM on 2025-09-17 after the buyer's owner called EOXS's ERP pricing "a money pit" — twice, in different words, across two separate proposal cycles (April 2025 full-implementation at ~$300K/yr, then a re-priced September 2025 attempt). Raj reopened contact in May 2026 with an AI-layer angle; the council's read is that this reopened warmth but never actually addressed the pricing-structure objection that killed the deal both times. The session ended with Raj and Claude drafting an outreach email to Charlotte Rasmussen (internal champion, the owner's granddaughter) pitching "Krinox" as a low-cost proof-of-value wedge ahead of any full ERP ask.

Separately, on 2026-07-30 Raj asked Claude to find an email where he'd recommended "Invera" to a prospect — surfacing an internal naming collision: Invera is EOXS's real ERP competitor (lost deals to it at Heidtman, Energy Alloys, Salt Creek/All Metals, Majestic), but Raj's own AP-automation module was also informally called "Invera" in at least one client pitch (to ACI/Sam Jenkins, May 2026) before being renamed "Invex."

On 2026-07-31, Raj asked for branding-expert input on whether to post a screenshot of a private email (from colleague Ryan, describing a conversation with a prospect referred to as "Tripp") to LinkedIn as a testimonial-style post. Claude advised against posting the raw screenshot on consent/trust grounds and proposed extracting the signal into an anonymized, self-authored post instead.

Two threads (2026-07-27) went nowhere substantively: a pricing-strategy conversation stalled because Raj's one-word answers ("sales") didn't disambiguate which pricing was meant (Krinox/Podar agent-fee model vs. IRIS at a specific account vs. core EOXS ERP pricing), and a landing-page psychology discussion (referencing Hawkins' Map of Consciousness and standard B2B conversion literature) was cut off awaiting Raj's clarification on scope (Krinox landing page vs. general). A final unrelated thread (2026-07-29) is a historical-trivia request for a "trusted advisor" figure (real, US-history, known to a small-company CEO, also a TV character) — Claude proposed Alexander Hamilton as the best real-world fit, and asked what the name was actually for, without getting a resolution in the transcript.
