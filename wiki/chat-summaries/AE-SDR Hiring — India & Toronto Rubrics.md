# AE-SDR Hiring — India & Toronto Rubrics

_Raj's build-out of screening rubrics, resume evaluations, comp benchmarks, and job descriptions for AskCru/AskCruz sales hiring across India (AE, SDR) and Toronto (SDR), plus adjacent GFC Intelligence inside-sales JDs and one tangential IRIS engineering-hiring discussion._

## Summary

Starting 2026-08-01, Raj worked through a multi-thread hiring push for AskCru/AskCruz (an AI operating system for industrial/distribution businesses, confirmed distinct from EOXS's ERP-to-steel-distributors business, though the assistant initially conflated the two before Raj clarified AskCru = AskCruz). The core rubric, built and reused verbatim across five separate threads (`ae-resume-screening-rubric`, `askcru-ae-hiring-india`, `askcru-ae-hiring-rubric`, `askcru-ae-hiring-screening`, `askcru-ae-india-hiring-rubric`, `askcruz-ae-india-hiring`) for the India-based AE role against ~300 LinkedIn applicants (later corrected to 61 for the Toronto SDR variant): must-haves are full-cycle quota-carrying closing experience, US-market selling experience, SMB/mid-market motion fit, and a quantified track record; hard red flags are high job iteration (6+ jobs/7 years or unexplained 10-14 month stints) and brand-chasing (logo-heavy resumes with no numbers). Screening order: tenure → brand-chasing → skills match → US-selling/SMB fit.

On 2026-08-17 the same rubric was adapted for a Toronto SDR role (thread `toronto-sdr-screening`): lower experience ceiling (want someone gradeable into AE in 6-12 months, so 5+ years of pure SDR work is itself a flag), a hard North America 10+ years / no-recent-immigrant filter Raj stated explicitly ("If I have to hire an Indian in Toronto, I will rather hire an Indian in India"), and a requirement for direct SaaS/software SDR quota experience (general sales does not qualify). One candidate (Keno Garcia) was screened and rejected on both software-experience and location grounds.

Comp benchmarking for the Toronto SDR-to-AE hire (2026-08-01, thread `toronto-sdr-ae-comp-benchmark`) landed on CAD $55K-65K base / $90K-115K OTE, 55/45-60/40 base-heavy split, reasoned as a "founding AE" comp pattern rather than a mature-playbook AE, since AskCruz has no proven US-outbound-to-close motion yet. This fed directly into a LinkedIn Sales Navigator sourcing plan for 3 Toronto hires against a stated $200K investment constraint ("Mission: let's build a Sales army").

23 individual AE candidate resumes were scored in thread `askcru-ae-hiring-india` (running leaderboard, one flagged for duplicated resume bullet text — Prerna Paswan — and one flagged for likely fabricated dates/metrics — Mohammed Abuzar Bangar). Individual full evaluations also appear standalone for Ashish Gupta (7/10, qualify with US/SMB gap unverified — thread `askcru-ae-india-hiring-rubric`), Rimpa Sen (4/10, disqualify on US-market fail despite strong ₹3Cr+ B2G/enterprise closing record — thread `askcru-ae-hiring-rubric`), and Arnold Stanley Dias (advance to screening call, strongest quantified resume in the batch, $550K ARR at Doctor Alliance US healthcare — thread `askcruz-ae-india-hiring`). A 9-question AE interview guide (docx, weighted scoring: sales fundamentals 25%, US market 20%, SMB fit 15%, client handling 10%, objection handling 10%, behavioral/tenure 15%, motivation 5%; 80-100 strong shortlist / 60-79 borderline / <60 reject) was built and delivered in thread `askcru-ae-hiring-india`.

Separately, Raj had the assistant draft inside-sales JDs for GFC/GFC Intelligence across four verticals (steel/building materials for AskCruz, plastics, lumber, chemicals — threads `gfc-inside-sales-jds` and `commodity-distribution-inside-sales-jds`), plus an India-based SDR JD requiring proven US-market selling experience. A 50ft market analysis (2026-08-17) recommended prioritizing steel + building materials/lumber as the strongest next verticals by market size/fragmentation, with the assistant repeatedly flagging that four unproven vertical hires in parallel (steel, plastics, lumber, chemicals) risked burning cash before any one motion was proven repeatable. A separate 50ft analysis proposed splitting the expensive "SDR" role in India into two cheaper BPO-sourced roles (Outbound Calling Executive ~₹22-35K/month, Lead Qualification Associate ~₹18-28K/month) instead, since the "SDR" title alone triggers a 2.5-4x pay premium versus BPO-equivalent skills.

## Tribal Knowledge Extracted
- **"High iteration" (Raj's term for job-hopping)** — his own shorthand for a candidate red flag: roughly 6+ jobs in 7 years, or a pattern of 10-14 month stints with no external explanation (acquisition, layoff, shutdown). Used consistently as a named first-pass filter across every AE/SDR rubric thread. (source: raj_2026-08-17_askcru-ae-hiring-screening.md, raj_2026-08-17_ae-resume-screening-rubric.md)
- **"Brand-chasing" (Raj's term)** — a resume that leads with recognizable employer logos but has vague, unquantified bullets ("responsible for enterprise accounts," "drove revenue growth"). Explicitly distinguished from a strong resume that happens to include a known company name plus real numbers — the latter is fine, the flag is logos-as-the-whole-pitch. Auto-disqualify criterion. (source: raj_2026-08-17_askcru-ae-hiring-screening.md)
- **Toronto hiring rule: "If I have to hire an Indian in Toronto, I will rather hire an Indian in India."** — Raj's explicit, unwritten-elsewhere reasoning for a hard 10+ years North America / no-recent-immigrant filter on the Toronto SDR role: a recent immigrant hired in Toronto isn't worth the Toronto cost premium over just hiring in India directly. This is a compensation/ROI rule, not (per his framing) a bias framing — worth preserving precisely since it explains a hard disqualifier that would otherwise look unmotivated. (source: raj_2026-08-17_toronto-sdr-screening.md)
- **AskCru = AskCruz** — the two names are used interchangeably by Raj for the same company/product (an AI operating system for industrial/distribution businesses); the assistant had to explicitly confirm this mid-thread since nothing in EOXS's own data connectors resolves "AskCru" as an entity. Distinct from EOXS's own ERP-to-steel-distributors business, which the assistant initially and incorrectly assumed AskCru was part of. (source: raj_2026-08-17_askcru-ae-hiring-screening.md)
- **India "SDR" title tax** — an unwritten market-structure fact surfaced during 50ft analysis: in India, anyone holding the title "SDR" prices 2.5-4x above a BPO/call-center agent doing functionally overlapping work (dial, qualify, book), purely because "SDR" postings pull from tech/SaaS-benchmarked applicant pools rather than BPO-benchmarked ones. The practical workaround Raj adopted: post under BPO-adjacent titles ("Outbound Calling Executive — International Voice Process") to pull BPO-priced applicants with comparable phone skills instead. (source: raj_2026-08-17_gfc-inside-sales-jds.md)
- **"Founding AE" comp logic** — the reasoning used to justify a base-heavy 55/45-60/40 split (vs. standard SaaS 50/50) for the Toronto SDR-to-AE hire: AskCruz has no proven US-outbound-to-close motion yet, so the hire is helping build the playbook, not executing a known one, and shouldn't be paid on a quota that was guessed rather than earned from historical deal-cycle data. (source: raj_2026-08-01_toronto-sdr-ae-comp-benchmark.md)
- **GFC's no-founder-salary-until-health policy** — an unwritten internal compensation constraint mentioned in the IRIS hiring thread: GFC founders draw no salary until the company reaches a health milestone, which sets a real ceiling against hiring market-competitive AI/engineering talent. Tangential to the AE/SDR cluster but the same underlying comp-discipline logic recurs in the founding-AE reasoning above. (source: raj_2026-07-27_iris-hiring-strategy.md)

## Key Points
- Rubric's 4 must-haves (AE, India): full-cycle quota-carrying closing (not SDR/BDR-only), US-market selling experience, SMB/mid-market motion fit, quantified track record on the resume itself.
- Rubric's 2 hard red flags: high iteration (6+/7yrs or 10-14mo unexplained stints) and brand-chasing (logos, no numbers).
- Screening order (AE, India): tenure/iteration → brand-chasing → skills/experience match → US-selling/SMB fit (last, as the final differentiator).
- Toronto SDR role additions (2026-08-17): hard disqualifier for <10 years in North America / recent immigrants; hard disqualifier for no direct SaaS/software SDR-BDR outbound quota experience (general sales doesn't count); target seniority band ~1-3 years SDR/BDR (5+ years pure SDR is itself a flag — overqualified/likely churn or stuck).
- Comp benchmark (2026-08-01, Toronto SDR-to-AE): CAD $55K-65K base, $90K-115K OTE, 55/45 to 60/40 base-heavy split; industry blended AE averages (Glassdoor ~$97K CAD, Wellfound ~$123K CAD) explicitly rejected as too high for an unproven-motion entry hire.
- $200K investment constraint stated by Raj for the Toronto sales-army hire; assistant flagged that 3 hires' base salary alone (~$180K CAD) plus payroll burden (~8-12%) would consume nearly the entire $200K before any commission is paid, before ramp-time productivity.
- 23 AE candidates scored in thread `askcru-ae-hiring-india`; flagged integrity issues: Prerna Paswan (duplicated bullet text across job entries) and Mohammed Abuzar Bangar (employment dates predate stated graduation year, suspiciously uniform metrics — fabrication risk).
- Individually scored candidates: Ashish Gupta 7/10 qualify (askcru-ae-india-hiring-rubric.md); Rimpa Sen 4/10 disqualify, strong ₹3Cr+ India enterprise/B2G closer but zero US exposure (askcru-ae-hiring-rubric.md); Arnold Stanley Dias — advance to call, $550K US healthcare ARR in year one at Doctor Alliance, strongest quantified resume in the batch, but two short (~5-6 month) stints to verify (askcruz-ae-india-hiring.md).
- 9-question AE interview guide built and delivered as docx (AE_Interview_Guide_AskCru.docx), weighted scoring 80-100/60-79/<60 shortlist bands, spanning HR/tenure, sales fundamentals, US market, SMB fit, client handling, objection handling, behavioral, and motivation categories.
- GFC/GFC Intelligence inside-sales JDs drafted for 4 verticals: steel/building materials (AskCruz), plastics (GFC Intelligence), lumber, chemicals — plus an India SDR JD requiring proven US-market selling experience for AskCruz.
- 50ft vertical-prioritization analysis (2026-08-17) recommended: keep steel/metals as proven anchor; building materials & lumber distribution as best next bet (largest, most fragmented); chemical distribution third (smaller but higher willingness-to-pay); a defined MRO sub-segment (e.g. industrial fasteners) fourth; electrical equipment/supplies fifth (caution: incumbents like Wesco/Rexel/Graybar already digitized at the top of that market). Plastics (GFC Intelligence's existing pilot, only 308 NAICS-verified companies) explicitly excluded from the "5 hot new verticals" as too narrow to be a standalone vertical.
- Assistant repeatedly flagged parallel-vertical-hiring risk: by the fourth vertical JD (chemicals), none of steel/plastics/lumber/chemicals had yet proven a repeatable close, meaning each new hire was a parallel unproven bet rather than a sequential validated one.
- India SDR cost-structure analysis: BPO international voice-process agents run ₹18,000-35,000/month vs. ₹58,000-100,000+/month for anyone titled "SDR" — proposed splitting the India SDR role into "Outbound Calling Executive" (~₹22-35K/month) and "Lead Qualification & Outreach Associate" (~₹18-28K/month) instead, recommending starting with the calling role only and proving conversion over 60-90 days.
