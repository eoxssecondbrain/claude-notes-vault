# Collier Metals — Account Overview & Call Prep

_Two conversations covering a full account pull on Collier Metals (an 11-month-dormant CRM lead) and prep/review for the Jul 30 2026 live call that followed, including transcript review, pricing-sheet critique, and reference-intro drafting._

## Summary

Collier Metals is an Atlanta-area steel service center (25-30 employees, 9.5 acres, two slitting lines/cut-to-length/edging lines, 95% secondary steel) running Microsoft Business Central + PS Data since 2015 for MTR/quality certs. The CRM lead (id 18771) was created 2025-08-28 off an SMU conversation between Rob Drum and Raj, bounced across owners (Yashvir Singh Thakur → Scott Begin same day → Rajat Jain 2025-09-02), and never moved past the "Leads" stage — 11 months of dormancy before any product demo happened. An Aug 29 2025 internal CRM note flagged Rob as "a potential ERP buyer" with a next step of Raj personally calling owner Tripp Collier; no evidence that call happened before 2026.

The first live demo happened 2026-07-20 (56 min, Fireflies-recorded), run solo by Ayan Dutta because Raj was hospitalized after a bike accident two days prior. Ayan covered MTR AI (live PDF extraction/spec-matching/NL chatbot demo) and the internally-codenamed "Turing"/Second Brain (C-suite only, not yet client-facing), positioning EOXS as an overlay on Business Central rather than a replacement, citing Gerdau, Sabre Alloys, and Brannon Steel as precedents. No pricing was discussed and no follow-up call was scheduled at that point — Rob was left to informally pitch Tripp internally.

A second call — "Collier / EOXS - AI Brainstorming" — happened 2026-07-30, prepped for in the second thread and then reviewed against its actual transcript and a resulting pricing proposal (Proposal_Collier_Metals_-_Quote.pdf) later that session. On the call: Tripp Collier (second-generation owner, took over from his father three years ago, bought the company from him last year, tripling revenue this year, shifting from toll-only to ~50/50 sales split) and Rob Drum both personally build their own AI automations using Claude (e.g., a slitter-tooling-setup automation reading emails/work orders, saving ~10 hrs/week). Rob asked about auto-importing mechanicals PDFs into PS Data; Raj referenced the Brannon Steel MTR precedent and Ayan gave a live 2-minute MTR AI demo. Pricing was discussed live (18 users, $350-500/user monthly range vs. $6,840 actual in the written proposal; implementation quoted live as $50-70k vs. $72,000/37 days in the proposal) alongside a verbal 4-week go-live promise carrying a $5,000/week delay penalty — a promise that contradicts the proposal's own ~7-8 week implementation timeline. Claude/AI integration pricing was also discussed live ($1,000 one-time per Claude account integration, $100/month pure pass-through — Raj's own words: "we don't make any money out of it" — plus separately priced agentic/custom work, e.g. ~$2,000 compute for a 6-hour deep prompt), none of which appeared in the written proposal at all.

A Council-mode review (GTM strategist, pricing/packaging, RevOps, enterprise sales motion, category positioning, facilitator/critic) flagged five issues, top of which was the complete absence of AI/Claude/MTR pricing from the proposal despite that being the deal's actual driver, plus a payment-schedule math error ($20k kickoff + $12k/month against $72k leaves an uneven $4k final month) and a missing next-call date (though the call was already calendared and accepted by Tripp separately). A follow-up-email draft was reviewed and revised: a soft correction line was drafted to walk the 4-week promise back to 8 weeks (justified as Raj having quoted 4 weeks based on an assumed 8-10 person training team vs. the actual 18), and the $5,000/week penalty clause was recommended against being written into the contract (risk of inviting advisor scrutiny and compounding the timeline correction into two retractions). Two separate reference-intro emails were drafted for Ryan Capinski (Eastern States Steel) — a solo briefing to Ryan first, then a joint intro with Tripp a day later — after pulling Raj's actual historical email tone with Ryan (short, direct, minimal preamble) from OV2 to match voice.
