# Team Habits, Hiring & Founder Ops (Aug 2026)

_Four loosely-related Raj threads from Aug 17–18, 2026 spanning a new hiring need (Implementation Consultant), a team-wide daily-Claude-usage habit mandate, an open-ended "how should I do today" check-in, and a GFC banking decision for a non-resident founder._

## Summary

**Implementation Consultant hiring (2026-08-18):** Raj opened a request to hire an Implementation Consultant with "full control over implementing software into companies," for EOXS's target customer profile (raw material distributors, 15–50 employees). The assistant noted this didn't cleanly match either the sales-strategist or raj-eoxs-vault skill, framed the role's stakes explicitly (this hire will make or break AskCruz's early client outcomes, which feeds directly into the company's $1M ARR retention/expansion goal), and began by calibrating depth/rigor before building out a job description — the thread ends at that calibration step, with no JD or rubric yet produced.

**Daily Claude-hour habit mandate (2026-08-18):** With the whole team now on Claude and connected to OV2, Raj wanted a 1–2 line standing instruction for everyone's Claude account instructions, mandating at least 1 hour/day of Claude usage for learning, research, or analysis. The assistant offered two framings (direct/mandate vs. growth-framed) and recommended the direct one specifically because it pairs the requirement with a logging ask — without some artifact (a log, tracker entry, shared output), the assistant warned the habit becomes unenforceable and would likely erode within two weeks.

**Open-ended daily check-in (2026-08-18):** Raj asked "how should i do today" with no further context. Rather than guessing, the assistant flagged that this doesn't map to a configured skill (not sales strategy, not a data pull, not an explicitly-invoked morning brief) and used `ask_user_input_v0` to force a choice among four concrete interpretations: prioritize using live data, general strategic sounding-board, calendar/schedule planning, or something else. No answer is recorded in this thread — it ends at the clarifying question.

**GFC banking for a non-resident founder (2026-08-17, 50ft rigor):** Raj needed a new US bank account for GFC (Ganesh Fruit Company, the AskCruz legal entity) after SVB — the bank EOXS already uses — declined to open one. Raj's core requirement: no in-person branch visit, since he isn't physically present in the US (a decade-old prior experience required exactly that). At 50ft depth, the assistant explained SVB's decline as a post-2023-collapse risk-appetite shift (now First Citizens Bank-owned, skews toward larger VC-backed accounts, not new small entities like GFC) — not a reflection on Raj specifically — then evaluated three non-resident-friendly options: Mercury (best fit — fully remote onboarding, passport-only KYC, no SSN/ITIN required, India not on its exclusion list), Relay (ruled out — requires SSN or ITIN, which Raj has neither), and Wise Business (not a real bank — an Electronic Money Institution useful only as a multi-currency payments add-on). The recommendation came with an explicit, documented catch: Mercury's frictionless onboarding is offset by a real pattern of sudden account freezes/closures during compliance reviews, sometimes with no stated reason, no appeal, and multi-week fund holds (a June 2026 BBB complaint cited a 60-day freeze with no violation ever named). The assistant recommended opening Mercury as the primary operating account while simultaneously standing up a lightly-funded Wise Business account as a fallback payment rail — set up now, not after a freeze happens — plus concrete freeze-avoidance practices (real business address not a registered-agent/PO box, account activity consistent with what was declared at signup, same-day response to document requests, no third-party pass-through payments). It flagged one blocking prerequisite: GFC's entity type (Delaware vs. Wyoming LLC) was still "considering," not finalized, and needs to be locked before any bank application since switching entity type after applying resets the process.

## Tribal Knowledge Extracted
- **Unenforceable habit mandates erode within about two weeks without an artifact** — the assistant's explicit reasoning for recommending a logging requirement alongside the 1-hour Claude mandate: a stated expectation with no verification mechanism (log, tracker entry, shared output) will get faked or quietly abandoned by the team on that rough timescale. This is the assistant's own operational judgment, not a documented company policy. (source: raw/claude-chat-queries/raj_2026-08-18_team-daily-claude-hour-habit.md)
- **SVB's non-resident-founder friendliness has structurally changed since its 2023 collapse** — it is still marketed as accepting non-resident founders with Delaware entities, but in practice now skews toward larger, VC-backed accounts; a new small entity like GFC without institutional backing is exactly the profile now declined. This is current, not historical, banking-relationship knowledge specific to EOXS's own experience (SVB already banks EOXS) and would not be discoverable from Mercury/Relay/Wise's own marketing. (source: raw/claude-chat-queries/raj_2026-08-17_gfc-us-bank-account-nonresident.md)
- **Mercury's real risk is post-onboarding, not onboarding** — the account-opening process is frictionless for non-residents, but the documented failure mode is sudden compliance-driven freezes/closures with weak or no explanation and long fund holds (up to 60 days in one cited case) — the opposite risk profile from what a "hard to open" bank would suggest. A company pursuing aggressive revenue targets (explicitly linked here to EOXS's $1M ARR goal) should treat a surprise freeze as a worse outcome than an occasional branch inconvenience, which directly shaped the recommendation to open Wise as a parallel fallback rail immediately rather than reactively. (source: raw/claude-chat-queries/raj_2026-08-17_gfc-us-bank-account-nonresident.md)
- **GFC's entity structure (Delaware vs. Wyoming LLC) was still unresolved as of Aug 17, 2026** — flagged as a hard blocker for the banking decision specifically because switching entity type after a bank application resets the whole process; this is a live open item, not a settled fact, and should not be assumed resolved without checking again. (source: raw/claude-chat-queries/raj_2026-08-17_gfc-us-bank-account-nonresident.md)
