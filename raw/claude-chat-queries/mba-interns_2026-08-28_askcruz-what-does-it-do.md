User: what does ask cruz do

Assistant: AskCruz (product family name "Cruz") is EOXS's AI-powered company knowledge base and conversational assistant — internally referred to as "Company Brain." It answers questions by pulling from connected sources:

- Email (Gmail, Zoho)
- Calls/meetings (Fireflies, Fathom)
- Client/implementation data (Odoo, implementation boards)
- Employee info (directory/HR sources)
- Internal docs (assets, policies, SOPs)
- Cross-source synthesis when a question spans multiple areas

It explicitly does not natively handle operational records/transactions (support tickets, invoices, sales orders) — that's routed through the separate eoxs-teams Odoo connection.

It's also being sold externally: it's in active sales motion with prospects like 3GM Steel and Sabre Alloys, pitched as the "Company Brain" product.

One flag worth your attention: the internal docs disagree on positioning. The product feature file describes AskCruz as steel-industry-specific (service centers, processors, tube mills, distributors), while the product-market-fit doc explicitly says it's industry-agnostic and warns against single-vertical positioning. The client-facing capability catalog sides with industry-agnostic. This wasn't reconciled as of the last update (Aug 25) — worth settling since it affects how it gets pitched to non-steel prospects like the legal-services one that's already in the pipeline.
