Here's my honest read, not flattery:

The story is a strong internal narrative for you. It's a weaker structure for a fair competition, and the two things need to be separated or the experiment will produce a result that looks meaningful but isn't.

Three real problems with "me vs 50 MBAs, straight PO dollar amount, six months":

1. Unfair baseline. You have ~20,000 LinkedIn connections, 10 years of steel industry relationships, founder authority, and can walk into any deal you've already half-closed through relationship equity. A 22-year-old MBA fresher has none of that. If you "win," the finding is "founder with a decade of relationships beats fresher with none" — which nobody needed a six-month experiment to prove. It doesn't actually test MBA vs non-MBA, it tests you vs them.

2. Attribution problem. Revenue in EOXS's sales motion has historically been founder-led, inbound and relationship-driven, not repeatable outbound. If a deal closes in month 4, how do you cleanly credit it to one intern's six months of activity versus warm-intro momentum that predates them? Without clean attribution the leaderboard becomes a story you tell afterward, not a number you can defend.

3. What are you actually trying to prove or produce? Those are two different designs:
   - If the goal is "prove MBA credentials don't matter" — you don't need 50 people or six months, you need one clean matched comparison and good PR.
   - If the goal is "generate real pipeline toward the $1M ARR target while running a hiring filter" — then revenue-per-intern is the wrong single metric; you want activity + conversion + revenue tracked in the task manager like you already planned, and hiring decisions come from that data, not from a dramatic reveal at month 6.

You already have the better mechanism designed — self-selected tasks in the task manager, KPIs per task, six-month performance data feeding a hire/no-hire decision. That's sound. The "I bet I can outsell all 50 of you" framing is a great opening speech, a bad scoreboard.

What do you actually want from me here — help designing the leaderboard/metrics so the comparison is real, or help scripting the day-one pitch to the interns, or something else?
