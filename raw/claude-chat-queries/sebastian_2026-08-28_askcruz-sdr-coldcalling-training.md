3. Steel jargon to know
General industry (say these correctly, don't need internal data for this):
- HRC / CRC — hot-rolled coil / cold-rolled coil
- Coil, sheet, plate — flat steel by thickness/form
- Service center — a distributor that buys mill-run steel and processes/resells it (your prospect)
- Toll processing — processing someone else's material for a fee, not owning it
- Slitting / cut-to-length — cutting coil into narrower coils or flat sheets to spec
- Mill cert — the certificate of chemical/mechanical properties that ships with material
- Tolerance — allowed variance from spec (thickness, width, camber)
- SKU — here, often means a specific grade+gauge+width combination, not just "a product"
- Ton vs. cwt — steel is priced by ton or hundredweight depending on region/product
AskCruz's own product vocabulary (useful once you're pitching the product itself):
- Open thread — an unresolved customer issue AskCruz can surface
- Promise date — the delivery commitment made to a customer
- Free to sell — inventory actually available once existing commitments are netted out
- True margin — margin after freight/processing costs are factored in
