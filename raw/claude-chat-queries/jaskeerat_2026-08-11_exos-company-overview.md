# EXOS / Making Steel Sexy Again — Company Overview

## User Query
"Tell me about exos" → "exos making steel"

## What EXOS Is

**EXOS** is the product/platform name from **EOXS** (legal entity: Prata Inc.), founded by Rajat Jain.

### Company Profile
- **Headquarters:** New Rochelle, NY
- **Tagline:** "Where Steel Meets Technology"
- **Founder:** Rajat Jain — claims 30 years of combined steel industry experience (founding team)
- **Market Focus:** Steel and metals distribution industry exclusively
- **Positioning:** The first AI-native ERP built exclusively for steel/metals, competing against generic vendors (SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Epicor)

### Product: EXOS ERP

EXOS offers **two distinct product tracks:**

#### 1. Core ERP Modules (Five modules)
- **Books** — Accounting & inventory
- **CRM** — Sales pipeline management
- **People** — HR and payroll
- **Shop** — E-commerce storefronts
- **Report Builder** — Business intelligence reporting

**Flagship feature:** "President's Cockpit" — consolidates all KPIs into a single executive dashboard.

**Pricing:** Starts at [restricted]; 15-day free trial available.

#### 2. AI Workers Suite (12+ standalone AI agents)
Task-specific agents marketed as **ERP-agnostic** — compatible with Invera, Stratix, SAP, Epicor, *and* EOXS ERP.

Named AI workers include:
- SmartQuote AI
- Steel Brain AI
- WiseQuote AI
- RevTrack AI
- Iris
- ViewStock AI
- SteelSynk AI
- AI Joe (Gerdau melt shop veteran persona)

"Making Steel Sexy Again" is both Raj's personal brand thesis (that the steel industry deserves modern, intelligent technology) and a published thought-leadership book.
