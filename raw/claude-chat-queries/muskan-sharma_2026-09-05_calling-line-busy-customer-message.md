# Calling Line Busy — Customer Message Draft

## User

I need you to draft a short, professional message for people who are currently unable to get through on our calling line.
The message should NOT mention anything about funds, credits, recharge, the AI agent not working, technical issues, or any internal problem.
We want to tell them that the line may be busy because multiple people are calling today for different queries. We want them to remain patient and reassure them that we will provide further updates.
The tone should be:
- Professional but natural
- Polite and reassuring
- Short and easy to understand
- Not overly apologetic
- Don't make any claim that we cannot confidently verify
The basic idea is:
"Hi, you may be facing this issue because a number of people are calling in today for different queries, which may be causing the line to be busy. Please be patient, and we will provide you with further updates shortly."
Please improve this wording and give me the best final version to send

## Assistant

Before the drafts, one flaw worth naming: you asked for no unverifiable claims, but the core line ("a number of people are calling in today") asserts a cause you can't verify and which, based on your own framing, isn't the actual cause. If a customer later calls at an off-peak hour and still can't get through, that story breaks and costs you credibility on something small.

You can avoid disclosing anything internal without asserting a false reason. Two versions:

Option A — recommended (no cause stated, nothing to disprove)

Hi, thanks for reaching out. Our calling line is experiencing higher-than-usual difficulty connecting today, so you may not get through immediately. We're on it and will share an update shortly. In the meantime, you can share your query here and we'll get back to you.

Option B — closest to your original (softened, if you want volume mentioned)

Hi, thanks for your patience. Call volumes can be heavy at times, so you may find the line busy today. We'll share an update shortly. If it's easier, drop your query here and we'll respond as soon as possible.

Notes:
- Both drop "please be patient," which reads as instructing the customer. "Thanks for your patience" does the same work without the ask.
- Adding a fallback channel is the single highest-value line here. Without it you're asking people to wait with no action available, which is where frustration and churn come from.
- "Further updates shortly" implies you will actually send one. If you won't, cut that line — an unfulfilled update promise is worse than saying nothing.
