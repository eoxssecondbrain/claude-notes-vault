### AskCruz Product Scope

**Four core capabilities:**
1. **Company Brain** — Shared working knowledge; answer business questions (e.g., "What did we promise this customer?")
2. **Company Hands** — Practical work (draft quotes, flag at-risk orders, summarize calls)
3. **Digital Workforce** — Standing responsibilities (watch queues, follow exceptions, report conditions)
4. **Company Personalization** — Reflects company-specific vocabulary, pricing, practices

**Functional domains:** Customers/accounts, orders & delivery, inventory & availability, quotes & pricing, calls & conversations, commitments, margins, operational issues.

**Key boundaries:** Not an ERP replacement. Access is role-aware. Doesn't automatically make every decision. Answers depend on available company information. Industry-agnostic (despite current steel-industry pilot/first client).
