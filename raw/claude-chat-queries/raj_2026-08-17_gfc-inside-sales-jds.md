ASSISTANT: Confirms your read on the numbers, then two role recommendations.

**The cost gap is real and it's structural, not perception**

International voice process / BPO agents in India: roughly ₹18,000–35,000/month (₹2.2L–4.2L/year), even in international outbound sales-flavored roles.

Anyone who carries the title "SDR" — even in India — prices in a completely different band: ₹58,000–100,000+/month (₹7L–12.8L/year median on Glassdoor, ₹10L average), because they're benchmarking against tech/SaaS pay, not BPO pay, and they know it. That's a 2.5–4x premium for someone whose core daily activity (dial, qualify, book) substantially overlaps with what a good BPO agent already does.

The lever isn't the person — it's the label and the applicant pool it pulls from. If a posting says "SDR" or "Tech Sales," you get SDR-priced applicants. If it says something that reads as a BPO-adjacent title, you get BPO-priced applicants with comparable phone skills.

**Two roles to post**

1. Outbound Calling Executive — International Voice Process (US Campaign)
Scope: high-volume outbound dialing into a US prospect list, script-guided qualification (fit/interest, not full discovery), booking meetings onto an AE's calendar. This is the "dial and book" layer, not full-cycle SDR work — no independent account research, no complex objection handling, no CRM strategy ownership.
Sourcing pool: international voice process / outbound sales BPO agents (Concentrix, Teleperformance, TTEC, WNS, Foundever alumni) who already have accent-neutral phone comfort and US-shift experience.
Pay band: ₹22,000–35,000/month base + per-meeting-booked incentive.

2. Lead Qualification & Outreach Associate (non-voice)
Scope: enriches account/contact data, runs written outbound (email + LinkedIn sequences), qualifies inbound chat/form leads, warms prospects before handoff to a closer. No cold calling.
Sourcing pool: BPO chat/email/non-voice support agents — strong written English, process discipline, lower phone-confidence bar, typically the cheapest tier in the BPO stack.
Pay band: ₹18,000–28,000/month.

Together these two roles replicate most of what a single "SDR" does, at roughly a third of the cost, and you can staff both for less than one SDR hire.

**Risk flags before you commit**

Attrition — voice-process BPO roles have some of the highest turnover in Indian white-collar labor (job-hopping for ₹2-3k/month raises is normal). Budget for a pipeline that replaces 30-40% of this headcount within the first year, not a one-and-done hire.

Ramp time — these candidates are strong at volume and script adherence, weak at unscripted value-based conversation (explaining what AskCruz actually does, handling "why not just use our ERP's built-in reporting"). If the calling script is tight and the qualification bar is simple, ramp is fast (2-3 weeks). If you expect them to run real discovery, ramp is much longer and some will never get there — that's the SDR premium you'd be paying for elsewhere.

Reputation risk — you're selling into an industrial/steel/plastics buyer network where you already lean on 20,000+ personal LinkedIn connections and company network as a distribution asset. A poorly-handled cold call from an underprepared caller costs more here than in a generic SaaS motion, because these are relationship-dense, referral-driven industries. Keep this role tightly scripted and QA'd, don't let it free-range on messaging.

Recommendation: start with role 1 only (voice/calling), prove the qualification-to-meeting conversion rate over 60-90 days, then decide if role 2 is worth adding or if the written-outreach work stays with existing team/AI tooling.
