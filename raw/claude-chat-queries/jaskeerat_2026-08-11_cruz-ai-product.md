# Cruz AI — Product Overview

## User Query
"What is Cruz AI?"

---

## What Cruz AI Is

**Cruz AI** (website: AskCruz) is EOXS's latest flagship AI product, launched in testing phase in **August 2026**. It was previously code-named **"Krinox"** and was renamed to **Cruz** / **AskCruz** in early August 2026.

### Official Positioning

From Rajat Jain's Aug 5, 2026 email:

> "Imagine this — for decades, only huge companies could afford a team of smart people like McKinsey to sit and analyze their business and tell them what to do next. Now imagine the same kind of expert analysis, except it runs on your laptop, costs a fraction as much, and never sleeps. That's Cruz."

**Core Value Proposition:**
- Cruz knows your business data
- Provides expert analysis 
- Helps you make the right decisions
- Runs locally on your laptop (no dependency on cloud)
- Affordable alternative to consultant firms like McKinsey

### Technical Details

- **Built on:** Claude (Anthropic's LLM)
- **Data Integration:** Connected to live EOXS business data — emails, call transcripts, support tickets, invoices, and internal wiki
- **Front-end:** Has its own dedicated web interface: https://cruz-dsvf.onrender.com/

### Market Scope

- **Originally positioned for:** Steel distributors and metals industry
- **Expanded to:** Any business with 20+ employees (as of Aug 5, 2026)
- **Not industry-specific** — the product fits general B2B operations

### Status & Rollout

**As of August 4, 2026:**
- **Testing phase** — internal EOXS team rollout announced
- Ayan Dutta (EOXS) sent announcement to all team members (Aug 4, 2026)
- Signup link: https://cruz-dsvf.onrender.com/
- Team instructed to use Cruz as default AI for all company-related work (except image/video generation and coding)

**As of August 10, 2026:**
- Client demo call conducted with Joe Brom (Ohio Strip Steel / formerly Greer Steel)
- Raj ran a screen-share walkthrough of the product

### Naming & Branding

**Product Name:**
- Official name: **Cruz**
- Website: **AskCruz**
- Rationale: "Cruz runs off the tongue similar to Alexa, Siri, etc." — single-word voice-activated AI brand

**Naming Feedback** (from Ryan Capinski, Eastern States Steel, Aug 5, 2026):
- Concern: "AskCruz sounds like Ask Jeeves that went out of business"
- Positive: Likened it to Alexa/Siri model
- Alternative suggestions discussed: "KalAI" (Superman reference), "CruzAI", "EOXS + CruzAI"
- Raj's response: Kept product name as "Cruz" but noted website is "askcruz"

### Previous Name

- **Krinox** — the original code-name for Cruz, mentioned in Raj's emails to Ryan Capinski (Aug 5, 2026): "We renamed Krinox to AskCruz"

### Internal Use Case

EOXS team is using Cruz for:
- Client questions
- Document drafting
- Research
- Planning
- Sales strategies
- General company-related intelligence

---

## Context: Evolution of EOXS AI Products

1. **EXOS ERP** — Core enterprise resource planning platform (launched ~2021-2022)
2. **AI Workers** — 12+ task-specific standalone AI agents (e.g., SmartQuote AI, Steel Brain AI, ViewStock AI, SteelSynk AI)
3. **Cruz / AskCruz** — Generalist "second brain" that integrates ALL company data and runs on Claude (Aug 2026)

Cruz represents a shift from **task-specific AI Workers** to a **unified, context-aware business intelligence assistant**.
