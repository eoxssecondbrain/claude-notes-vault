## AskCruz Proactive Features

**User:** Suggest 5 features that could make Ask Cruz proactive instead of only responding when employees ask questions

**Assistant:** Before I suggest features, I need clarity on what you mean by "proactive" and what AskCruz looks like today. Right now I have baseline context — zero customers, zero revenue, reset target of $250–500k ARR by Aug 31, 2027 — but that's not enough to suggest good features. I'm missing:

1. **What does proactive mean to you?** Are you thinking:
   - AI agents actively pushing knowledge/alerts to employees without them asking (Slack notifications, email digests)?
   - Predictive suggestions (detecting what an employee might need to know based on context)?
   - Scheduled briefings or onboarding sequences?
   - Something else?

2. **Current state of the product.** What's the typical user flow today? How do employees access it, and what kinds of questions are they asking (or likely to ask)?

3. **Target users.** Who are we building for? (e.g., onboarding new employees, knowledge workers in a specific department, entire organizations?) What's the use case that will drive $250–500k ARR?

4. **Business outcome from proactivity.** What do you want proactivity to drive? Engagement/usage time? Faster onboarding? Reduced support tickets? Better decision-making? Retention?

5. **Constraints.** Any hard limits — technical debt, integration dependencies, privacy/data governance, team capacity? Anything that would kill certain feature ideas?

6. **Competitive angle.** Are you positioning proactivity as a differentiation point from competitors, or is it just expected table stakes?

What's the one or two of these I should start with?
