User: What exactly is Cruz? What are we building, how are we building it, what it will do, who will it cater, everything about it. Explain it to me like I haven't heard of it.

Assistant: AskCruz is the flagship product of Ganesh Fruit Company (GFC), your separate holding company built alongside EOXS. Full picture below.

**Where it came from**

GFC is named after your grandfather's 50-year fruit business in India. The product itself went through two name changes: Podar, then briefly Krinox, and it now permanently sits as AskCruz — website askcruz.com. Krinox should not be used going forward. "Cruz" is the named AI agent persona that lives inside the product; when the product talks to a customer, it talks as Cruz.

**What it actually is**

Positioning is explicit: an AI operating system for businesses, not a services or consulting company. The mechanism is a Claude account wired into an Obsidian Vault (always called "OV" internally) that continuously ingests:
- live ERP data
- real-time email
- call transcripts
- a static knowledge base layer

That combination is what makes it an "operating system" rather than a chatbot — it has a running, current picture of the business, not just a document store.

AskCruz is a superset of IRIS. IRIS itself is the live ERP + Claude query layer (natural-language BI over live ERP/CRM data) — the IP for IRIS is being formally acquired from EOXS for $10,000 and transferred into GFC, so AskCruz owns it cleanly rather than licensing it.

Separate and important: Zola is a different, EOXS-only R&D tool (a live-call AI assistant demoed to Sabre Alloys) — not part of AskCruz. Don't conflate the two.

**What it does — the capability stack**

The product vision is laid out in layers, roughly in order of sophistication:
Company Brain → Company Hands → Digital Workforce → Generic Intelligence → Professional/Human DNA → Company Personalization → Marketplace → Continuous Learning

In plain terms: it starts as a system that knows everything about the business (Brain), then can act on that knowledge (Hands), then scales into multiple specialized agents doing real work (Digital Workforce), then generalizes and personalizes to the specific company and even specific professional roles/personas (Human DNA — this is the same lineage as the "AI Joe" Gerdau-veteran-cloning concept from EOXS), and eventually opens into a marketplace with continuous learning.

One concrete product concept under this: Agent Vinod — an agent that ingests all of a client org's daily Claude conversation threads, summarizes them, and builds a searchable knowledge base/wiki so a CEO can ask "what has my team been talking about this month" or "what problems is my team hitting."

**Who it's for**

ICP is the mid-market CEO or P&L owner — someone who can say yes to a $30K deal without a committee. GTM does not lean on SEO at all; it's outbound sales plus social content, using your existing 20K LinkedIn connections and six company profiles as the distribution asset. The funnel CTA was deliberately changed from "waitlist" to "book a fit call" — signaling sales-led, not product-led growth. Revenue target: roughly 30 clients at $30K ACV within three months of launch.

Sabre and Monarch Steel are the two accounts intended as first real AskCruz builds. SA2 at Sabre is explicitly only an IRIS-style pilot, not a full AskCruz deployment — don't overstate that one.
