Assistant: A stress test is checking how a system behaves under extreme or worst-case conditions, not normal ones — to find its breaking point or weak spots before they show up in production.

Depends on context:

- **Software/systems**: pushing load, traffic, or concurrent users far beyond expected levels to see when/how it fails (crashes, slows down, data corruption).
- **Finance**: modeling how a portfolio, bank, or company would perform under a severe adverse scenario (market crash, rate spike, mass defaults).
- **Physical/engineering**: applying extreme force/heat/pressure to a material or structure to find failure limits.
- **Medical**: monitoring heart function while the body is under exertion (treadmill test) to reveal problems not visible at rest.

Common thread: normal testing checks "does it work correctly," stress testing checks "when and how does it break."
