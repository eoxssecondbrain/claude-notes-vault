Three font directions for "Where Steel Meets AI":

1. **Industrial Serif meets Mono** — Set "Where Steel Meets" in a heavy slab serif (like Rockwell or Zilla Slab Bold) and "AI" in a thin monospace/terminal font (like JetBrains Mono or Space Mono). The contrast literally embodies the phrase — old industrial weight crashing into digital precision.

2. **Distressed Industrial Stencil** — Full phrase in a stencil font (like Army Stencil or Bebas Neue with a stencil treatment), slightly worn/distressed texture like it's stamped on a steel beam. Rugged, foundry feel. Logos underneath in clean sans for contrast.

3. **Glitch/Circuit Serif** — A classic bold serif (Playfair Display or a custom-cut steel-plate serif) for "Where Steel Meets," with "AI" rendered as if it's glitching or dissolving into circuit-line fragments/pixels. Visually shows steel literally transforming into AI.

My pick for a conference crowd (steel execs + tech curiosity): option 1 — it reads instantly and doesn't risk looking gimmicky on a shirt at a distance.
