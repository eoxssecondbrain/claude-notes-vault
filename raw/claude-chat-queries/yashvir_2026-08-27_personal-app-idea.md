User: can i use it on my phone also?

Assistant: Yes — open the same link on your phone's browser and it'll work exactly the same way (it's a normal web page, not something installed from an app store).

One thing to be clear on though, given you picked local-only storage: your phone and your desktop will each have their own separate, unsynced data. Logging a meal on your phone won't show up if you open the link on your laptop — each device's browser keeps its own local copy. If you mainly want to use this on your phone day-to-day, that's fine as-is. If you want it to work across both devices with the same log, that's the sync/account layer I flagged earlier — a different, bigger build.

To get it onto your phone like an app icon: open the link in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android), then use "Add to Home Screen" from the browser's share/menu button. It'll launch full-screen from there without browser chrome.
