# Jagriti - AskCruz Product Overview

## User Query
"Give me a brief explanation of askcruz and what does it do?"

## Response Summary
AskCruz is an AI-powered company knowledge platform that converts scattered business information (emails, calls, client data, support tickets) into a searchable, conversational assistant.

### Key Capabilities:
- Collects and synthesizes business information from multiple sources
- Provides natural-language Q&A interface backed by evidence
- Offers summarization, comparison, timelines, and analysis
- Applies role-based access controls to information

### Current Status:
- Internal pilot at EOXS (parent company)
- First external client: 3GM (mid-implementation)
- Industry-agnostic platform

### Value Proposition:
Eliminates manual searching across dispersed information sources by providing a centralized, AI-assisted "company second brain."

### Jagriti's Role:
Working on the frontend of teams.askcruz.com as a startup generalist—involved in building the chat interface and user experience layer.
