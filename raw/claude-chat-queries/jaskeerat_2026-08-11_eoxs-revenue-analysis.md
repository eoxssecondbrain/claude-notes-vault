# EOXS Revenue & Pricing Model Analysis

## Key Finding: Pricing Is Confirmed But Amounts Are Redacted

The vault **confirms** EOXS uses per-user monthly SaaS billing, but **all dollar amounts are [restricted]**.

## Confirmed Pricing Model

**Per-Seat Monthly Billing**
- Each user costs a fixed amount per month
- Users must be archived to stop billing
- Monthly invoicing (evidence: INV/2026/0049, INV/2026/0271, etc. — amounts redacted)

**Module-Based Tiers**

1. Full ERP (Sabre, 3GM, Discount Pipe, PPC, RW Conklin)
   - Includes: Inventory, Processing, Accounting, CRM, Sales
   
2. Sales + CRM Only (Eastern States Steel)
   - No Inventory/Processing/Accounting
   - Explicitly lowest-tier deal in ICP
   
3. CRM-Only (Greer/Ohio Strip Steel)
   - Sales + relationship management
   - Tested at 8 users with 30% discount
   
4. AI-Only (Brannon Steel, Jan 2026)
   - MTR (Material Test Report) AI product
   - Not traditional ERP subscription

**Standard Contract Terms**
- 3-year term
- 4% max annual renewal increase
- Exceptions: Eastern States (1-year), discounts for early signers
