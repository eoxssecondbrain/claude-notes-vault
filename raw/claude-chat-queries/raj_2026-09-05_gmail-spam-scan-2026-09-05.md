## Why the run stopped at 350 threads (not exhaustive)
Gmail's search semantics mean `-label:AI-Reviewed` matches a **thread** if *any single message* in it lacks the label — even if every other message in that thread already carries it. Because this mailbox has tens of thousands of historical threads and the label was applied incrementally over time, this query kept surfacing large swaths of already-reviewed historical threads (each with one newer/appended message not yet labeled) rather than just genuinely new unprocessed mail. After 7 pages (350 threads) with a 100% NOT_SPAM skip-list hit rate and no new signal, continued pagination looked like it would keep going through most of the mailbox's history for no benefit, so the run was stopped early. More matching threads likely remain (next pageToken captured: `13895217773436192290`).
