Assistant: Researched Cattr mobile options. Found:
- Android app "Cattr" (net.amazingcat.cattr) on Google Play, last updated Sep 2021, still v0.1-BETA
- iOS app also existed (v1.0.0, released 2021) from Amazingcat LLC
- Both described as offering "location capture and activity detection" - NOT the same as desktop's screenshot/keyboard/mouse activity tracking
- iOS/Android block apps from monitoring activity in other apps at the OS level, so full activity-tracking parity with the desktop client isn't possible on mobile regardless of app
- Mobile app hasn't been updated since 2021 - flagged risk that it may be broken or removed from app stores; recommended testing before company-wide rollout

Conclusion given to Ron: mobile app exists for clock-in/out + location, but won't replicate desktop-level activity tracking (OS restriction, not a Cattr limitation), and the app is stale/unmaintained.
