User: Give me the three main pain points from the steel industry

Assistant: From the EOXS ICP documentation (source: ICP asset, cross-checked against wiki entries on steel distributor deals):

1. Legacy systems not built for the industry. Their ERPs weren't designed for metals-specific workflows — coil tracking, cut-to-length, heat/certs, mill test reports. They're running general-purpose software bent to fit a steel business, not the reverse.

2. Manual, disconnected processes. Inventory, pricing, and order management run through spreadsheets, email, and separate systems that don't talk to each other — no real-time visibility across branches, which is the single most cited pain point in deal-specific wiki entries (e.g., Greer Steel, Energy Alloys evaluations).

3. Margin pressure from leaner, larger competitors. Mid-market distributors need to compete against bigger players who already have better tooling — the operational drag from points 1-2 directly compounds this.

One caveat: this is the EOXS ERP pain-point set (the product's original ICP). AskCruz itself is positioned industry-agnostic — its pitch is data fragmentation generally, not steel-specific — with 3GM as its only steel client so far. If you're cold-calling for AskCruz specifically, pain point 2 (fragmented data, no single source of truth) is the one that maps directly onto AskCruz's actual pitch; 1 and 3 are more EOXS-ERP-flavored and worth knowing but not the AskCruz hook itself.

User: Summarize then for a cold call

Assistant: Cold-call framing, steel industry pain points:

Opener angle: "Most steel distributors we talk to are still running ERP systems that weren't built for the industry — no native support for coil tracking, cut-to-length, heat certs, or mill test reports. Is that something you're dealing with?"

Three pain points to probe for, in order of how commonly they land:

1. Fragmented visibility. Inventory, pricing, and orders live in disconnected systems or spreadsheets — no real-time view across branches. This is the strongest AskCruz hook since it's literally the product's pitch (scattered data, single place to ask).

2. Legacy, non-metals ERP. Systems bent to fit steel workflows instead of built for them. Good pain point to surface but it's more an EOXS ERP angle than AskCruz's — use it to build rapport/credibility, not as the AskCruz close.

3. Losing ground to bigger, leaner competitors. Use as a consequence, not a lead — "the manual work is costing you time your bigger competitors don't lose."

Lead with 1, use 2 to show industry fluency, close with 3 as the stakes.
