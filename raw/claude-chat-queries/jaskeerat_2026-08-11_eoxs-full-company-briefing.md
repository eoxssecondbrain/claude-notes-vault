# EOXS Full Company Briefing

## What EOXS Does

EOXS builds cloud-based ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) software for the steel and specialty metals industry. The core product is a SaaS platform that handles operations, inventory, billing, and supply chain management for steel distributors and processors.

Key product lines:
- Core ERP platform (subscription-based, multi-user licensing)
- MTR AI (materials tracking & analytics add-on)
- IRIS (CCO dashboard)
- AI Workers (automation tools)
- Implementation & consulting services
