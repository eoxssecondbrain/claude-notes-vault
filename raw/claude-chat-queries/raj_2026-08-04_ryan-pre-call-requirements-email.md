Three things on the thinking behind it.

The instinct is right for the wrong-sounding reason. The value isn't mainly that it "helps the AI" — it's that a written request is the cheapest artifact in a services business. It becomes the scope boundary, the ticket, and the thing you point at later when the ask has quietly grown. The OV/AskCruz ingestion benefit is real but secondary.

The bigger gap isn't the email. It's that you're walking into Thursday without knowing whether this is in-contract support or paid custom development. Ryan is your highest-trust client contact and an informal sales partner for you — which is exactly the relationship where feature requests get absorbed for free by default and nobody ever names it. Decide your commercial position before the call, not during it.

Pick the variant based on that. The structured version gets you a better spec but reads like a ticket form to someone who behaves more like an ally than a customer. The light-touch version protects the relationship tone and still gets you 70% of the information.

I can check OV2 for his recent correspondence to see if the ask is already telegraphed somewhere — which would mean you go into Thursday informed regardless of whether he replies. Rigor options if you want that:

50ft — the above plus contract/scope position, billing history, and a read on whether this should be priced, so you have the commercial answer ready before he asks.
