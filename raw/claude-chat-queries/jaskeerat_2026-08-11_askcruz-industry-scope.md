# Does AskCruz Only Work for Steel Industry?

## Short Answer

**Currently positioned for steel, but architected to scale beyond it.**

---

## Current Launch Positioning

AskCruz is *built specifically for steel right now:*

- **ICP:** Mid-market steel and specialty metals distributors
- **First pilots:** Sabre Alloys, Monarch Steel (from your EOXS roster)
- **GTM:** Leverages your 20K LinkedIn connections + six company profiles (all steel-focused)
- **Data integration:** Starts by querying EOXS ERP instances (steel-specific)

**Why steel as launch point:**
1. Domain expertise (you run EOXS in steel)
2. Existing customer base and relationships
3. Credibility with decision-makers
4. Larger platforms (Palantir, Foundry) don't serve this niche well

---

## The Architecture Supports Multi-Vertical Expansion

The capability roadmap includes:

- **Generic Intelligence** (layer 4) — "learns and generalizes across clients"
- **Marketplace** (layer 7) — "connects agents and services"
- **Continuous Learning** (layer 8) — "adapts over time"

These suggest the system is **industry-agnostic** once mature. If you can build Company Brain + Hands + Agents for a steel distributor, the architecture works for food distribution, logistics, manufacturing, any mid-market B2B.

---

## The Strategic Trade-Off

From the $50M vs. $1B path analysis:

> "Path B abandons the steel-network moat that made Path A safe."

**Path A ($50M, 5 years):**
- Stay focused on steel
- Build defensible vertical monopoly
- Keep EOXS relationship intact
- End state: $2-5M ARR profitable business, full ownership

**Path B ($1B, 5 years):**
- Expand beyond steel into horizontal "AI OS for any mid-market CEO"
- Lose concentrated domain advantage
- Compete against Palantir's strengths (general-purpose AI platform)
- Higher risk, higher reward

**The "moat" is real:** Steel gives network effect (more clients → better product for all → harder to displace). Generic OS plays against larger competitors' strengths.

---

## Likely Expansion Path (Inferred)

**Year 1-2:** Steel industry focus
- 30 clients at $30K ACV
- Build credibility and proof points
- Develop repeatable delivery playbook

**Year 3-5:** Adjacent verticals (if Path B chosen)
- Logistics (shipments, customers, supply chain)
- Manufacturing (orders, inventory, operations)
- Food/beverage distribution (your GFC heritage)
- Potentially any mid-market B2B services/distribution

Expanding kills the steel moat. Staying focused keeps defensible position and $2-5M ARR floor.

## Bottom Line

AskCruz is **steel-first, not steel-only.**

- Launches in steel because you have customers, credibility, and domain knowledge
- Architecture supports expansion to any mid-market vertical
- Strategic choice: stay in steel for defensible $2-5M ARR, or expand horizontally to chase $1B but compete against larger AI platforms on their turf
