Section 1 — Nursery: what is it

Strip away the marketing language and Sales Navigator is one thing: a paid, filtered version of LinkedIn's own member database, built for finding and tracking specific people at specific companies, instead of just browsing your feed. Under the hood it's basically a large database of everyone on LinkedIn, searchable and filterable based on the data people put on their profiles — a more advanced version of the standard LinkedIn search.

Regular LinkedIn (even Premium) is built for browsing your network and seeing content. Sales Navigator is built for the opposite job: finding people you don't know yet, at companies you've chosen, and reaching them even outside your network. It gives you advanced search filters, deep insight into leads and accounts, and ways to connect with decision-makers outside your current network. Think of "leads" as individual people you're tracking, and "accounts" as the companies those people work at — that distinction becomes important in the next section.

Why it exists at all: LinkedIn has roughly a billion members across 200 countries, and almost none of that is searchable in a useful way through the free product. Sales Navigator gives precision targeting within that database using filters that aren't available in LinkedIn Basic or Premium. That's the entire value proposition — not new data, just a much sharper way to cut through data LinkedIn already has.

One honest caveat worth knowing on day one, since it changes how you'll use it later: it finds people, it doesn't hand you their email or phone number. Sales Navigator deliberately withholds contact data — you can export lead lists with names, titles, companies, and LinkedIn URLs, but not email addresses or phone numbers. That's a separate tool's job, and we'll get to where that fits in later sections.
