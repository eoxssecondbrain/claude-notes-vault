# AskCruz: Comprehensive Briefing

## What is AskCruz?

AskCruz is an AI operating system for mid-market businesses. Not a chatbot, consulting service, or traditional software — it's a Claude-powered agent that runs a live, current picture of your business and acts on it.

**Parent company:** Ganesh Fruit Company (GFC) — your separate holding company alongside EOXS.

**Product structure:**
- Core: Obsidian Vault (OV) continuously ingesting live ERP data, email, call transcripts, static KB
- Brain: Claude queries unified vault for current business picture
- Agent: "Cruz" persona answers and acts on data
- Superset: Includes IRIS (natural-language BI over ERP/CRM, acquired from EOXS for $10K)

---

## How AskCruz Works

**The mechanism:**

1. Ingestion — all business data feeds continuously into Obsidian Vault
   - Live ERP (orders, inventory, customers)
   - Real-time email
   - Call transcripts (Fireflies, Fathom)
   - Static knowledge base

2. The Brain — Claude queries unified vault for current (not stale) business picture

3. Agent Actions — Cruz can answer questions, generate reports, surface insights, escalate actions

4. Personalization — adapts to company's specific roles and workflows

**Differentiator:** Has a real, live, current picture of business — not a chatbot searching old documents.

---

## Business Problems It Solves

For mid-market CEO/P&L owner:

1. Information fragmentation — answers live across ERP, email, calls, KB without hunting systems
2. Slow decision-making — natural-language beats dashboard navigation
3. Delegation gap — AI agents can summarize team discussions, flag problems, escalate
4. Knowledge loss — captures and makes searchable all conversations (Agent Vinod: summarize threads monthly)
5. Operational visibility — real-time status across all systems in one interface

**Target ICP:** Mid-market CEO/P&L owner who can approve $30K deal solo.

**Example use case:** Sabre Alloys — ask "Which customers are at risk?" or "Why is that order stuck?" and get answers by querying live ERP, tickets, email in natural language.
