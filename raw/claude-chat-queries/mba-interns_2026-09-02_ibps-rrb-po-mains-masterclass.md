## User

can you make mains complete mock papers as an expert exam setter ?

## Assistant

Yes, but three constraints shape what's actually worth building.

GA cannot be mocked for this exam. Roughly 65% of that section is current affairs from July–December 2026. December hasn't happened, and my reliable knowledge ends in May 2026. Any GA section I write would be either stale or invented. A mock with a fabricated GA section is worse than no GA section, because it trains recall of things that were never asked. That part has to come from a live test series or a monthly capsule.

The quality bottleneck is puzzle and DI verification, not writing. A 15-person double-entity puzzle or a caselet DI set has to have exactly one consistent solution. Generating them is easy; guaranteeing uniqueness requires solving each one computationally before it ships. An unverified puzzle set with two valid arrangements or an unsolvable constraint is actively harmful practice — you'll spend eight minutes concluding you're bad at puzzles when the puzzle was broken. This is the entire difference between craft levels here.

A static PDF is not a substitute for a paid test series. A test series gives you a timed CBT interface, percentile against thousands of candidates, and section-wise analytics. None of that comes from a document. What I can do better than a test series is unlimited targeted sets on the Mains-specific formats that free and Prelims-oriented material underserves — critical reasoning, machine input-output, caselet DI, idiom-in-dialogue, word swap. That is the highest-leverage use of this.

So my recommendation is sectional sets on those formats rather than a full 200-question paper. But it's your call.
