# EOXS Revenue — Last 3 Years

**User question:** Give me the revenue of EOXS (last 3 years)

**Assistant answer:**

Bottom line: there is no single audited/official revenue figure in the vault. EOXS is privately held and doesn't file consolidated financials; what exists is a series of dated, self-reported figures from investor pitch decks, internal emails, and a draft tax filing — and they don't fully reconcile with each other (the vault itself flags this as an open contradiction).

## 2023
- Quarterly traction (SaaS/Implementation): Q2 $8,000/$32,800; Q3 $12,600/$33,700; Q4 $14,900/$57,400 (Apr 2023 investor pitch deck)
- Full-year 2023 (stated Jan 2024): $268K ARR + $200K implementation revenue, pitched to Mucker Capital for $2M seed
- Internal 2023 recap: OpEx cut to $1,500/mo; $20K raised from angel; deals closed: Morgan-Hauser, HTX, Sabre Steel

## 2024
- Full-year 2024 revenue: $172K (Tax Filing 1120 draft, 2025-09-12)
- 2024 projected ARR (internal sales deck): $228K — same deck claims 360% YoY growth in 2023 and 38-person team, which the wiki itself flags as an internal contradiction (low ARR vs. high growth claim)

## 2025
- 2025 YTD revenue (as of Sept 2025): $250K YTD (same tax draft)
- Mid-2025 ARR (disclosed to prospective investor CreativeCo/Christian Czentye): $400K ARR + $150K signed-unbilled + $800K pipeline; 120+ offshore team; 1:2 OpEx-to-revenue ratio
- Investor's benchmark to re-engage: $30-40K MRR / $360K-500K ARR — implying EOXS was just under that range in July 2025

## 2026 (partial)
- No full-year 2026 revenue figure in vault yet (through late July 2026 ingest)
- Known active-client MRR sums to ~$27-30K/month (Sabre Alloys ~$11K, 3GM Steel ~$4.8K, Discount Pipe & Steel ~$3.1K, PPC Metals ~$2.75K, Eastern States Steel ~$2.3K, Ohio Strip Steel ~$1.7K, Brannon Steel ~$0.9K, RW Conklin ~$0.45K) — this is client-instance MRR only, excludes implementation fees, IRIS/AI Workers add-ons, Gerdau/Hansen subcontracting revenue.

## Why figures don't reconcile
- Different purposes (investor pitch vs. tax draft vs. internal recap), different definitions (ARR vs trailing revenue vs YTD vs projected)
- 2024 figures conflict internally ($172K tax draft vs $228K projected ARR deck)
- No consolidated/audited P&L exists; Prata Inc. (EOXS's Delaware C-Corp) reportedly had zero federal tax filings (Form 1120/5472) for 2020-2024 as of Sept 2025, only began cleanup with outside accountant (Counto) around that time.
