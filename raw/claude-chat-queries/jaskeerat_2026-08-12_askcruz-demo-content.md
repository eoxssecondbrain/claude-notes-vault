# AskCruz Demo Content & Video Script

## Demo Question (Privacy-Safe)

### **"Tell me everything about our engagement with a mid-market manufacturing client — their contract terms, recent implementation issues, and payment history."**

---

## How AskCruz Answers (Generic Example Flow)

**Step 1: Pull Contract & Client Data**
- Search client records: "Find all contracts signed in 2024"
- Result: Contract dated March 2024, 3-year agreement, $50K/month SaaS + implementation

**Step 2: Check Recent Implementation Activity**
- Search call transcripts: "Latest discussion with this client"
- Result: Nov 15 call about inventory module deployment, 3 outstanding bugs

**Step 3: Cross-Reference Invoices & Payments**
- Search billing: "All invoices this year, payment status"
- Result: 12 invoices issued, 11 paid on-time, 1 pending (15 days overdue)

**Step 4: Identify Risk Signals**
- Search emails: "Recent client complaints or escalations"
- Result: 2 support tickets marked "urgent" last month

**Answer Synthesis:**
Client is generally healthy (11/12 on-time payments) but showing early stress signals (1 overdue payment + 2 urgent tickets). The Nov 15 call suggests implementation friction. Recommend relationship check-in before next renewal.

## Key Visual/Production Notes

**Color Scheme:**
- Dark background (professional, not cluttered)
- Highlight AskCruz answer in **brand color** (whatever your primary is)
- Red/amber warning indicator for "RISK" signals

**Graphics:**
- Clock animations for time savings (30 min → 8 seconds)
- Smooth screen transitions (don't jump around)
- Text appears as voiceover speaks (reading + hearing = better retention)

**Tone:**
- Not hyper, not robotic — confident and calm
- Feel like a busy executive's dream tool, not a gimmick

**B-Roll Suggestions:**
- Professional office setting
- Hands typing on keyboard
- Split-screen: "before" (chaos) vs. "after" (clarity)
- If using actors: mid-40s executive, tired/stressed → then relieved
