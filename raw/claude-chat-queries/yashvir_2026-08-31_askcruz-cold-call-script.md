User: mention atleast 3 common pain points and then draft 3 different scripts for each common pain points

Assistant: Three pain points chosen for standalone scripts, picked for how broadly they apply rather than how much data backs them: quoting delays and inventory blind spots hit nearly every steel distributor regardless of size, since both come down to the same root cause — pricing and inventory data split across systems. Lost commitments (what got promised, and by whom) is the third because it compounds with the skilled-labor and turnover pressure already common in the industry — when a rep leaves, the record usually leaves with them.

Script 1 — Quoting delays

Cold opener: "Hi [Name], it's Yash — I'll be straight with you, this is a cold call. I've got one specific question about how fast your team gets quotes out. You got 20 seconds?"
[pause]
If "go ahead": "I've been looking at how [Company]'s sales team handles quoting, and I had one specific question. Got 30 seconds?"

Gatekeeper: "Hi, I'm trying to reach whoever runs sales at [Company] — is that you, or is there a Head of Sales or Sales Director I should be talking to?"
If pushed: "Sure — I had a specific question about how your team's quote turnaround works, and wanted their take directly."

Reason for call: "Most sales leaders I talk to at steel distributors deal with the same thing — quotes taking longer than they should because pricing and inventory data live in different systems. I wanted to ask how that shows up for your team, if at all."

Discovery, Situation: "Walk me through how a quote actually gets built today — is pricing and inventory info in one place, or scattered across a few systems?"
Discovery, Problem: "When a rep needs to get a fast quote out, how long does that usually take end to end?"
Discovery, Implication: "When it takes longer than it should, what does that cost you — a slower cycle, a customer who goes elsewhere, a rep chasing down numbers instead of selling? Is that daily, or occasional?"
Discovery, Need-payoff: "If quote turnaround stopped being a bottleneck, what would that be worth to your numbers this quarter? Who else feels this — your reps, your ops team?"

Insight: "What I've noticed with distributors like yours — the real cost isn't the minutes spent building the quote, it's the deals that go to whoever quoted faster, even when the price was comparable. Speed itself is the differentiator, and it's rarely tracked as one."

Bridge: "That's the exact problem AskCruz is built around for sales teams — a rep can ask a plain question and get pricing and inventory in one answer instead of hunting across systems, so quotes go out faster. Given what you just described, I think it's worth a real look."

Close: "Is this something you'd evaluate yourself, or does it need GM sign-off at your company?" / "Is this something you're actively trying to fix, or more of a someday problem?" / "I'd like to show you how this would work against what you just described — 20 minutes this week or next?"

Voicemail: "Hi [Name], it's Yash — calling about your team's quote turnaround, not a general check-in. I'll try again, but my number's [number] if you want to skip the back-and-forth."
