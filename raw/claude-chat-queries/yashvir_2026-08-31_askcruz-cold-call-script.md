Script 1 — CXO / Owner / President

Cold opener
"Hi [Name], it's Yash — I'll be straight with you, this is a cold call. I've got one specific question about how decisions get made at [Company]. You got 20 seconds?"

Gatekeeper path
"Hi, I'm trying to reach [Name] — is this something you'd handle, or is [Name] the right person for a question about company-wide visibility?"
If pushed: "Sure — I had a specific question for [Name] about how information moves across departments at [Company], and it's not really a call I can do justice to secondhand."

Reason for the call
"The reason I'm calling — most owners I talk to run into one of two things: a customer's margin moves and nobody's fully sure why, or a lot of how the business actually runs lives in one or two people's heads. I wanted to ask how that shows up at [Company], if at all."

Discovery — Situation: "When you need an answer that crosses department lines — sales and ops, say — how easy is that to actually get today?"
Discovery — Problem: "When a customer's margin changes, how do you find out why?" / "If your most experienced person left tomorrow, how much of what they know is written down anywhere?"
Discovery — Implication: "When you don't have that visibility, what happens — do decisions get made anyway, just without the full picture?"
Discovery — Need-payoff: "If you could get a straight answer on either of those without chasing someone down, what would that be worth?"

Insight
"Here's what I've noticed talking to owners in your position — the real risk usually isn't that the information is slow, it's that decisions get made without it because nobody wanted to wait for the full picture. That's the cost that doesn't show up on a report."

Bridge
"That's actually the exact problem AskCruz is built around — it lets you or anyone on your leadership team ask a plain question and get an answer grounded in what the company actually knows, instead of it living in one person's head. Given what you just described, I think it's worth a real look."

Close
"Is this something you'd want to look at directly, or is there someone else who'd need to be in that conversation too?"
"I'd like to show you how this would work against what you just described — 20 minutes this week or next?"
