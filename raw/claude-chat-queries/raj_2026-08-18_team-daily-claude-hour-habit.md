Assistant: Two options depending on tone you want:

**Direct/mandate framing:**
"Spend a minimum of 1 hour daily on Claude — learning, research, or analysis that adds value to your work or the company. Log what you explored."

**Growth-framing (softer, same requirement):**
"1 hour/day minimum with Claude — use it to learn, research, or solve something relevant to your role. This is a standing expectation, not optional."

I'd go with the first — it's specific (minimum, daily) and asks for a log, which gives you a way to actually verify compliance instead of trusting it happened. Without some artifact (a log, a task tracker entry, output shared somewhere) this becomes unenforceable and people will either fake it or let it slide within two weeks.
