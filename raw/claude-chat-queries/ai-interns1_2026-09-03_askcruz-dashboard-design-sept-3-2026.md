AskCruz Management Dashboard Design (Sept 3, 2026)

ARTIFACT: Interactive dashboard for tracking AskCruz progress toward $250-500k ARR goal (12-month target ending Aug 31, 2027)

KEY SECTIONS:
1. ARR tracker: current ($0) vs target ($375k midpoint), months remaining (11), monthly run rate needed ($34k)
2. Sales pipeline: 18 prospects identified → 2 active trials → 1 proposed deal (Sabre) + 7 qualified prospects in next 4 weeks → $180k pilot pipeline potential
3. ARR projection: conservative month-by-month model showing path to $375k assuming 2-3 deals convert by Apr-May, 60% trial-to-paid conversion rate
4. Unit economics: CAC ($8-15k), contract value ($75k median ARR), LTV/CAC ratio (15-28x vs 3x breakeven), gross margin (78-82%), trial conversion target (60%), net retention projection (105-115%)
5. Value creation table: 5 concrete metrics where AskCruz creates measurable business value:
   - Sales acceleration (inquiry response time, close rate lift)
   - Sales intelligence (deal velocity, win/loss analysis)
   - Operational efficiency (time per task, manual-to-automated)
   - Customer retention (expansion revenue, ACV growth)
   - Team capability (rep productivity, training time reduction)
6. Critical success factors & risk signals: must-haves by end of Q4 2026, red flags to watch

HOW TO USE:
- Weekly review: bring to all leadership syncs (Rajat + Ron + team)
- Update pipeline as trials progress or stall
- Move Sabre from "Proposed" to "Signed" once contract closes
- Escalate any trial exceeding 8 weeks
- Track CAC and trial-to-paid rate weekly against benchmarks ($30k max, 60% target)

GROUNDING IN REALITY:
- Dashboard assumes Sabre closes by end of Q4 2026 (validates 50ft analysis Opportunity 1: AI Workers standalone pricing/sales motion is real)
- ARR projection is conservative: only 2-3 deals needed to hit midpoint ($375k), not 10+ small deals
- Unit economics show strong margin, which means you can afford CAC as high as $30k/deal and still break even within 1 year
- Value creation table is specific to Sabre use case (sales acceleration, deal scoring, automation) — this becomes your repeatable story for next 5 pilots

METRICS HIERARCHY:
1. Pipeline → ARR (what matters for the goal)
2. Trial-to-paid conversion rate → unit economics feasibility
3. CAC → scale efficiency
4. Usage data from paying customers → retention and expansion risk

All other metrics are noise until you have paying customers.
