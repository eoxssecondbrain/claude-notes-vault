User: Is it possible to upload videos on Fathom to get the transcript for the same? Or can this be done on Fireflies.

Assistant: This question doesn't match either skill (threads-ov is for saving transcripts, raj-eoxs-vault is for EOXS internal data) — it's a plain product-capability question about Fathom/Fireflies. Answering directly.

No on Fathom, yes on Fireflies.

**Fathom:** Right now, Fathom can only record and transcribe live meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams — it does not support uploaded calls. No transcript can be generated without a live recording through one of those platforms.

**Fireflies:** only Fireflies can transcribe uploaded files — it accepts audio and video uploads, including MP3, MP4, M4A, and WAV, which helps with calls recorded elsewhere.

So if you have a video file recorded outside a live Zoom/Meet/Teams call (e.g. a client screen recording, a phone-recorded meeting), Fireflies is the one that can transcribe it — Fathom can't touch it.
