Assistant: Here's the diagnosis and the fix.

Why it's stuck

- 25+ people, 15 minutes. If everyone spoke, that's under 40 seconds each. Open mic can't scale to this — it will always default to the same 3-4 confident people, no matter how many times you ask "anyone else?"
- Mandatory attendance and camera-on gets you presence, not participation. Those are different things. People can be sitting there, camera on, saying nothing, for weeks.
- "Any feedback?" is a vague prompt. Vague prompts favor people who think out loud easily. Everyone else freezes because they don't know what counts as worth saying.

The fix: change the format, not the pep talk

Four exercises, pick one or rotate:

1. Round robin, one line, hard cap
Fixed order (not "who wants to go"), one fixed question, 20-30 seconds each, timer running. Everyone answers the same question: "one thing I found today." At 25 people x 25 sec, that's about 10 minutes — fits the window. No volunteering, no freezing. Order removes the bottleneck.

2. Pair up first, report back after
5 min: random pairs, each person tells their partner what they found.
10 min: pick 3-4 pairs to share the best nugget with the whole group.
This gets all 25 people actually talking (just not to the full room), and 1:1 conversation does more for "feeling connected" than broadcasting to 25 faces ever will.

3. Write one line before the call
Everyone drops one line in a shared channel or doc before the 45 minutes ends: what they found. Live 15 minutes is spent picking 3-4 interesting ones and asking the person to expand. Lower bar than speaking cold — people who won't raise a hand will type a sentence.

4. Rotating spotlight roster
Assign 2-3 people per day, on a visible rotating schedule, to give a slightly longer 2-minute share. They know in advance, so they can prepare instead of getting put on the spot. Over 2-3 weeks, everyone gets a turn.

Start with option 2 or 3 — they solve both your goals at once (talking + connection) without asking 25 people to perform on the spot.

Risks worth flagging

- Making all 25 speak individually every single day won't hold up. Rotate who gets airtime rather than trying to fit everyone in daily.
- Mandatory plus camera-on can produce performed compliance — people who look engaged but contribute nothing real. Watch for this, don't just count attendance as success.
- If any senior leadership is on this call, that alone suppresses junior voices. Worth checking whether that's part of what's going on here.
- You're bundling two different goals — brainstorming output and team connection — into one 15-minute slot. They may need separate formats on different days rather than one format trying to do both.

How to check if it's working
Track unique speakers for two weeks, not just "did the meeting happen." If the same 3 names keep showing up, the format hasn't actually changed anything yet.
