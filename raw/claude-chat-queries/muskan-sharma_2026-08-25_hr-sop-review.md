1. Recruitment & Hiring
Purpose: To manage candidates and hiring activities through Odoo. Steps
1. Go to Recruitment → Applications. 2. Select the relevant job position. 3. Create a candidate/application. 4. Add the following candidate details: – Candidate Name – Email – Phone Number – Resume – Applied Position – Source
5. Move the candidate through the recruitment stages: – New – Screening – Shortlisted – Interview – Selected – Offer – Hired / Refused
6. Record interview feedback. 7. Once selected, complete the required documentation. 8. Create the employee profile after confirmation of joining.

• Aadhaar/ID or required identification
• Employment Agreement

4. Leave Management
Purpose: To manage employee leave requests and approvals through Odoo. Employee Process
1. Go to Time Off. 2. Click New / Request Time Off. 3. Select leave type. 4. Select dates. 5. Enter the reason if required. 6. Submit the request.

5. Attendance Management
Purpose: To maintain accurate employee attendance records. Process
1. Go to Attendances. 2. Monitor employee check-in/check-out records. 3. Review missing or incorrect attendance. 4. Coordinate with the employee where clarification is required. 5. Correct records only when authorized. 6. Review attendance regularly for payroll and reporting purposes.

6. Working Hours & Cattr Monitoring
EOXS and AskCruz use Cattr for tracking employee working hours. HR Process
1. Ensure every employee has a Cattr account. 2. Assign the appropriate project/task. 3. Confirm that the employee can successfully track time. 4. Review Cattr records periodically. 5. Compare working-hour data with HR records where required. 6. Record relevant observations in the employee's HR/log records. 7. Follow up on significant discrepancies with the employee and manager.

7. Payroll Management
Purpose: To ensure employee salary information is maintained accurately. HR Process
1. Review employee salary details. 2. Check attendance and approved leaves. 3. Review unpaid leave or other applicable deductions. 4. Verify joining/resignation dates. 5. Calculate applicable salary. 6. Prepare salary slips. 7. Verify salary information before processing. 8. Maintain payroll records securely.

8. Employee Transfer / Promotion
Process — When an employee changes their role, department, manager, or salary:
1. Obtain management approval. 2. Update the employee profile in Odoo. 3. Update the following details: – Job Position – Department – Manager – Salary, if applicable – Working Hours
4. Record the effective date. 5. Upload the promotion/transfer letter. 6. Inform the relevant teams.

9. Resignation Process
Process
1. Receive the employee's resignation email/letter. 2. Record the resignation date. 3. Confirm the employee's Last Working Day (LWD). 4. Check the applicable notice period. 5. Obtain management confirmation regarding: – Notice period – Early release – Immediate acceptance
6. Update the employee's status/records. 7. Start the offboarding process. 8. Coordinate final salary/F&F. 9. Collect company assets and access information. 10. Issue Experience/Relieving Letter when applicable.

10. Termination Process
Process
1. Obtain authorized management decision. 2. Prepare the termination letter. 3. Communicate the termination professionally. 4. Record the employee's last working day. 5. Process salary/F&F according to the applicable company process. 6. Collect company property and information. 7. Ensure the employee logs out of all company devices/accounts. 8. Ensure ongoing tasks/projects are handed over to the TL or Manager. 9. Update the employee's status in Odoo. 10. Store the termination documentation securely.
