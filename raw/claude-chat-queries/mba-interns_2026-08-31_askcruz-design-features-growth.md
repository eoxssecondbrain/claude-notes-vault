**Assistant:** Seven design skills are available. What each does, one line on AskCruz:

design:user-research — plans interview guides, usability tests, survey design. For AskCruz: talk to churned or hesitant trial users to find the actual objection blocking signups/conversions, instead of guessing.

design:research-synthesis — takes interview transcripts, support tickets, NPS responses and turns them into patterns and prioritized next steps. For AskCruz: if you're already sitting on support tickets or call notes, this turns that pile into a ranked list of what to fix first.

design:design-critique — structured feedback on usability, hierarchy, consistency for a screen or flow. For AskCruz: sanity-check the signup/onboarding flow or pricing page before you ship it, catch friction before real users hit it.

design:ux-copy — microcopy, error messages, empty states, CTAs. For AskCruz: sharper CTA and onboarding copy is one of the cheapest, fastest levers on conversion — no dev time required.

design:accessibility-review — WCAG audit: contrast, keyboard nav, touch targets, screen reader behavior. For AskCruz: matters more once you have real traffic volume or enterprise buyers who ask about compliance; low leverage pre-revenue.

design:design-system — audits naming/consistency, documents component variants, extends a design system. For AskCruz: only relevant once you have an actual product with a UI library and more than one person building on it.

design:design-handoff — generates dev-ready specs (tokens, props, breakpoints, states) from a design. For AskCruz: useful once design and engineering are separate people/steps; if you're still one person shipping fast, this is overhead.

Straight read: for a pre-$1M-ARR product where the goal is inbound/organic growth, ux-copy, design-critique, and user-research are the ones with real near-term leverage — they touch conversion and retention directly. Design-system, handoff, and accessibility-review are solving problems you don't have yet; they become relevant once you have a team and scale, not before. Research-synthesis is only useful if you already have a volume of user feedback sitting unanalyzed — worth checking if that's true.
