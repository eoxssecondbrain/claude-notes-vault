Two things to flag before you send this:

The ₹30,05,600 assumes a flat rate for all 5 years, no raises, and 52 working weeks a year with zero missed weeks. If Hashir's base pay goes up, or he takes leave, the real number moves. Worth deciding now whether the vault locks the rate at today's ₹578/hr or recalculates as his base grows.

A single 5-year cliff with no visibility in between is a hard sell for retention — most people won't trust a number they can't check on.
