# 3GM Steel Pricing Overview

## Question
Who was the first client and how much do we charge them?

## Findings

### First Client Identification
**3GM Steel** is the first client, with implementation beginning in **April 2022**:
- Earliest implementation tasks dated April 26, 2022 (TASK00015 onwards)
- Kick-off call initiated with multiple discovery and setup tasks
- First call on record: May 12, 2023

### Pricing Structure

#### Initial Implementation (2022)
- **First Invoice (S00008):** $29,120.00
- **Description:** Kick Off - 50% implementation fee
- **Date:** April 19, 2022
- **Status:** Paid
- **Included Services:** 
  - Discovery
  - Design
  - Data Review
  - Process Review
  - Customization
  - Server Setup
  - Instance & Domain Setup
  - Access Rights
  - GAP Analysis
  - Training

#### Current Subscription Model (2025)
- **Monthly Subscription:** $4,759/month
- **Annual Cost:** ~$57,108/year
- **Licensed Users:** 15 office users
- **Cost Per User:** $423/user/month
- **List Price:** $6,345/month (before discount)
- **Applied Discount:** 25% sign-up discount
- **Contract Term:** 3 years (signed July 31, 2025)
- **Renewal Terms:** Max 6% increase allowed at contract renewal

#### Included Modules
- Sales Assist & CRM
- Inventory Management
- Accounting Management
- Contact Management
- EOXS Dashboard Reports
- EOXS-OPS Mobile Reporting

### Contract Notes
1. Subscription includes licenses and product maintenance (all pricing in USD)
2. Single point of contact required between client and EOXS team
3. All users must be licensed; additional users in increments of 2
4. Fixed pricing for 3-year contract term
5. Additional users added at $423/user/month

### Summary
3GM Steel evolved from an upfront implementation deal ($29K) in 2022 to a robust SaaS subscription model ($4,759/month or ~$57K/year) with a 3-year commitment as of July 2025.
