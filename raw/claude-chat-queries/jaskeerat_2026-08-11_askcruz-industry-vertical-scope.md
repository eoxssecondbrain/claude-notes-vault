# Is AskCruz Steel-Only? Industry Vertical Scope

## The Answer: No, AskCruz is Industry-Agnostic

AskCruz is a general-purpose **AI operating system for mid-market businesses** — applicable to any vertical.

### Core Positioning

> "AskCruz is an AI operating system for businesses, not a services or consulting company."

The mechanism (Claude + Obsidian Vault pulling live ERP data, email, calls, KB) works for any business that:
1. Uses an ERP system (any vendor, not just EOXS)
2. Has a CEO/P&L owner with $30K budget authority
3. Has messy, siloed data across multiple systems

---

## Why Steel as the Launch Beachhead?

Strategic choice, not technical limitation:

1. **You know steel** — EOXS domain expertise, 8 existing clients, credibility
2. **Low friction entry** — Sabre Alloys, Monarch Steel are existing EOXS customers; first pilots easier
3. **Defensible moat early** — Build steel-specific agents (supplier risk, margin rules by product type, mill certifications) competitors without domain knowledge can't replicate
4. **GTM leverage** — 20K LinkedIn connections likely steel-heavy; six company profiles are steel-focused distribution asset

---

## What Scales to Other Industries

**ICP is agnostic:**
> "Mid-market CEO or P&L owner — someone who can say yes to a $30K deal without a committee."

**Not:** "Steel industry CEO"  
**Not:** "Metal distributor with EOXS"

Just: "Mid-market CEO with operational pain."

**Capability stack is vertical-agnostic:**

---

## The Defensible Moat (in Steel, Portable to Others)

Domain-specific agents built for steel:

1. **Pricing agents** — margin rules, coil vs. bar vs. plate dynamics
2. **Supplier risk agents** — geopolitical supply chain flags for steel
3. **Compliance agents** — import tariffs, mill certifications, trade laws
4. **Customer behavior agents** — seasonal patterns (construction, automotive)

These *could* be ported to other verticals (automotive, food, pharma, SaaS, CPG), but require rebuilding domain expertise. That's your defensibility in Y1-2.

---

## The Expansion Path

**Year 1:** Laser focus on steel ($30 clients at $30K ACV in 90 days). Prove model in vertical you dominate.

**Year 2-3:** Once delivery and unit economics proven, expand to other mid-market verticals (automotive, food, pharma, B2B SaaS, CPG).

**Year 5:** Generic vertical-agnostic AI OS for any mid-market business.

But you don't start generic. You start in steel where you have unfair advantage, then expand.

---

## Precedent Pattern

- Slack: Launched in gaming company, scaled to every industry
- Figma: Started in crypto/NFT circles, now omnipresent in design
- **AskCruz:** Launched in steel (EOXS customers + your domain expertise), scales to any mid-market vertical

---

## Bottom Line

AskCruz works for any mid-market business with an ERP system. Steel is the launch beachhead, not the ceiling. You're using existing EOXS footprint and domain expertise to get early revenue and prove the model, then expanding vertically once delivery and GTM are proven.
